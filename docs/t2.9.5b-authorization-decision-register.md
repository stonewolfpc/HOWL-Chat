## 2. Verbatim decision questions from the conversational record

### AQ1 — 5A test/API conflict

> How should the 5A A5 test be handled?  
> The package cannot compile after the signature change, and the existing legacy-ASR subtest asserts no deadline, contrary to L7. Options: authorize a minimal migration of that subtest to `TranscribeAudioFile` with the 15-minute deadline; authorize another explicit compatibility approach; or revise the frozen interface. No 5A test change is proposed without this decision.

**Verified evidence:**

- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:958` places the transport contract in T2.9.5B.
- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:963-967` says the 15-minute `audioTranscriptionTimeout` belongs to T2.9.5B and applies only to `TranscribeAudio` and `TranscribeAudioFile`; TTS must not inherit it.
- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:1130` records the 5A A5 expectation that TTS and the temporary ASR bridge have no artificial deadline.
- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:1140` requires the 15-minute deadline for both transcription entry points while keeping TTS and voice enumeration out of that policy.
- `app_audio_ownership_test.go:247-248` defines the A5 test, and `app_audio_ownership_test.go:263` calls `app.TranscribeAudio("clip.wav")` using the old signature.
- `app-audio.go:55` is the current one-argument `TranscribeAudio(audioPath string)` binding.

**Closure constraint:** AQ1 must not be resolved by silently making TTS inherit the transcription timeout. Any migration must preserve the TTS and voice-enumeration zero-timeout behavior.

### AQ2 — Base64 format and cap

> Is the wire base64 padded or unpadded?  
> Use the frozen `ceil(4/3 × MaxWAVBytes)` value of **25,600,059** with raw/unpadded base64, or use standard padded base64 with **25,600,060** and record the correction? The current frontend’s likely `btoa` output is padded, so this affects T1/T2.

**Verified evidence:**

- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:257-265` defines the ordered limits and the `ceil(4/3 × MaxWAVBytes)` formula but does not state padded versus raw/unpadded representation.
- `frontend/dist/js/audio-service.js:78` currently produces a WebM blob.
- `frontend/dist/js/audio-service.js:127` currently calls the old string-path binding.
- `frontend/dist/js/audio-service.js:149` creates an object URL; it does not establish a base64 encoder.
- `frontend/wailsjs/go/main/App.d.ts:243` still declares the old string binding.
- No `btoa` call or WAV/base64 encoder was verified in the reviewed frontend path. The question’s “likely `btoa`” clause is preserved verbatim but is not treated as established evidence.

**Closure constraint:** AQ2 must settle the actual wire representation. It is not sufficient to change only a numeric constant. The chosen representation and cap must agree with the encoder and decoder behavior.

### AQ3 — `MaxDecodedSeconds`

> What is `MaxDecodedSeconds`?  
> Is it exactly 600 seconds, or derived from `MaxRecordingMs / 1000`? The frozen text names the constant but gives no number.

**Verified evidence:** `docs/asr-repair-design.md:260` names the check but supplies no numeric value or derivation rule.

### AQ4 — T6 margin

> How is T6’s “2× margin” calculated?  
> Does “below `MaxBase64Chars` with a 2× margin” mean the measured ceiling must be at least twice `MaxBase64Chars`, or a different comparison? This determines whether T6 passes, requires a cap reduction, or is BLOCKED.

**Verified evidence:** `docs/asr-repair-design.md:263-265` states the 2× margin wording, and `docs/asr-repair-design.md:1103` makes T6 a packaged-runtime measurement gate. The comparison is not mechanically defined.

### CL1 — Imported-file copy

> Should the imported path copy into a per-request temp directory?  
> This proposal does so to keep the Whisper sidecar out of the user’s source directory. The design’s D8 wording supports binding ownership, but the imported paragraph does not explicitly say “copy.” Please confirm.

**Verified evidence:**

- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:213-226` establishes the imported-file policy and limits but does not explicitly say to copy the source file.
- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:799-812` establishes two temporary-directory lifetimes and says the per-transcription directory is created by the binding before writing `input.wav`; that supports binding ownership but does not by itself require copying an imported source.
- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:958` includes the imported-file policy and binding temp work in 5B scope.
- `internal/audio/asr/whisper/recognizer.go:305` derives the sidecar beside the input. The sidecar risk is real, but the copy remains an `[I]` recommendation pending CL1.

### CL2 and CL3 — WAV parser details

> Are `fmt ` before `data` and `fmt ` lengths greater than 16 accepted?  
> The frozen table says “at least 16” and “no optional chunks,” but does not state chunk order or whether extra `fmt ` bytes are allowed. This proposal accepts one `fmt ` and one `data` in either order, with `fmt ` length ≥16, and ignores extra `fmt ` bytes. If canonical order/length 16 is intended, that must be stated.

**Verified evidence:**

- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:216` requires no optional chunks beyond `fmt ` and `data`.
- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:245-252` requires a strict chunk walk, exactly one `fmt ` and one `data`, `fmt ` length ≥16, and the duration rule.
- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:249` records the “length ≥ 16” rule but does not say whether extra bytes are accepted.
- The design does not state chunk order. CL2 therefore resolves order; CL3 resolves extended `fmt ` handling. Neither is closed by this register.

### MEAS1 — Measurement responsibility

> What is the T6 measurement owner/environment?  
> The stage may close with T6 BLOCKED, but packaged-runtime readiness cannot be claimed. Who provides the packaged runtime measurement, and when?

**Verified evidence:**

- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:958` requires the payload ceiling to be measured or the gate blocked.
- `docs/asr-repair-design.md:1103` defines T6 as measurement on the packaged runtime with a documented margin, or a blocked gate.
- `STAGE_STATUS.md:1817` permits closure with a documented T6 **BLOCKED** gate but forbids claiming packaged-runtime readiness without measurement.

**Administrative closure:** MEAS1 may be closed by assigning an owner and environment, or by recording an explicit `T6 BLOCKED` disposition. The latter is not a readiness claim.
