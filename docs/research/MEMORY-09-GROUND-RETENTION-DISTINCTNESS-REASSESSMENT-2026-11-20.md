**PGR-04 (Calculated — what "meaningfully distinct" has to mean to be answerable).** Two positions are **distinct as
statements** if they require different things. They are **distinct as choices** if picking one over the other changes

— a difference that a prior decision already forces is a compliance question, not a choice. Both senses are tested
separately (§4), because they do not have the same answer, and conflating them is how the original three-position
framing went wrong (CGE-36).

## 2. What the classification settles, and what it leaves

**PGR-05 (Source-derived — settled).** The cause is **not** a ground under another name (ODC-14; CGE-20). Whatever
else follows, one of the two branches GRP-21 left open is now closed.

**PGR-06 (Source-derived — additionally settled).** The cause, when an actor supplied it, **is also a report of that
actor's stated rationale** (ODC-06) — recognised, not reclassified, and not required to be stored separately
(ODC-28/29).

**PGR-07 (Observed — left open).** Whether that **rationale** is itself a ground remains undecided (ODC-18). This
record treats it as undecided and does not lean on either answer (§4.3).

**PGR-08 (Calculated — and the decision cannot be used to close it).** The obvious shortcut is to argue that a
reported rationale is not a ground *because* ODC-07 says it establishes nothing. That argument fails: **a ground
establishes nothing either.** A ground is first-person testimony about the act, authored by the party whose act it
explains, and it "can raise auditability… but cannot by itself raise reliability" (TGP-20; GRP-09). "Establishes no
truth, authority or validity" therefore **does not discriminate** report from ground — it describes both. So ODC-07
constrains what the content may be used for without settling what the content is called. That is exactly the residual
ODC-18 named, and this record does not spend it.

**PGR-09 (Calculated — the one thing the decision does for axis B).** GRP-30's axis B records that P-G0 "requires
that cause not be a ground". That precondition is now **met** (PGR-05). P-G0 is therefore no longer exposed to
GRP-21's first branch — the branch in which P-G0 "is not a description of the corpus but a claim about vocabulary"
that "would have to displace an earlier owner decision to be true". **Narrowed:** GRP-21's conditional resolves to
its second branch, and GRP-50 item 4 ("P-G0 and P-G1 coincide only under an unsettled identification") is
**superseded in part** — the identification is now settled on the cause side while the coincidence question moves to
the rationale (PGR-07).

**PGR-10 (Calculated — what that does not buy).** P-G0 is not thereby *available* in the sense of being free of
obligations it would need to displace. It would still have to be read against **D-3b's reach into lifecycle
transitions**, which is **candidate-level, not established** (TGP-11; GRP-51). Availability moves; it does not
arrive. This is the single most consequential line in the record, and §5 returns to it.

## 4. Are they still meaningfully distinct?

**PGR-16 (Calculated — the question has three senses, and they are not the same question).**

| Sense | What it asks | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| **S-1 statement** | do the two positions require different things? | **yes** — see PGR-17 |
| **S-2 content** | would the streams they produce differ? | **yes, by one artifact** — and that artifact is already owed (PGR-18) |
| **S-3 ground** | is the difference a difference **about grounds**? | **no, unless ODC-18 resolves toward the rationale being a ground** (PGR-19) |

**PGR-17 (Calculated — S-1: distinct as statements).** P-G0 requires nothing about basis. P-G1 requires the cause on
statement-less transitions. These are different requirements, and no reading of ODC makes them the same sentence.
Anyone asked "which do you adopt?" is asked a real question. **The positions are not equivalent, and this record does
not find them equivalent.**

**PGR-18 (Calculated — S-2: distinct in content, by an artifact that is already required).** The streams differ by the
cause artifact on the statement-less class — CGE-40's "always differ by one artifact", whose *character* ODC-16 fixed
(a cause that also reports a rationale) and whose *size* remains unmeasured (CGE-29/30). The distinguishing point is
that this artifact is **not new**: it is D-3b's M-1, an obligation the owner already decided (GRP-23; ODC-30).

**PGR-19 (Calculated — S-3: the difference is not, as such, about grounds).** Two branches, and neither makes the
choice a ground choice:

| Branch | P-G0 retains | P-G1 retains | Is the difference about grounds? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ODC-18 → rationale is not a ground** | no ground | no ground (a cause and its report) | **no** — both retain zero grounds; the difference is the cause artifact |
| **ODC-18 → rationale is a ground** | no ground | **one** ground, on the statement-less subset | **yes** — this is GRP-21's structure, now carried by the rationale rather than the cause |

**PGR-20 (Calculated — so the answer is conditional, and the condition is named).** P-G0 and P-G1 are **distinct as
statements in both branches**. They are **distinct as choices about grounds in only the second**. Under the first,
choosing between them does not decide whether a ground is retained — it decides whether an already-decided cause
obligation reaches lifecycle transitions. That is a **scope question about D-3b** (TGP-11), not a new retention
policy, and it is the question P-G0's availability actually rests on (PGR-10).

**PGR-21 (Inferred — the consequence for how the option space should be described).** Naming the choice
"no ground vs. D-3b's floor" now over-describes it. Under the first branch, both positions retain no ground, and the
label "ground retention" applies to neither; under the second, they differ in ground content exactly on an
**unmeasured subset** (CGE-29/30) and the difference is a **classification consequence**, not a coverage decision
made by the chooser. The honest description in both branches is: *P-G0 is silence about the cause on lifecycle
transitions; P-G1 applies D-3b's cause obligation to them.* That description is available from text and asserts no
preference.

**PGR-22 (Observed — what "not equivalent" and "not a ground choice" can both mean).** They are consistent: the
positions differ in what they require (PGR-17) *and* neither is a policy about retaining grounds (PGR-19). The
earlier record's claim that they were "neither equivalent nor adjacent" (GRP-21) was correct about equivalence and
rested the non-adjacency on an unsettled identification; that identification is now settled on the cause side, so
**the non-adjacency claim is narrowed**: they remain non-adjacent in content, but no longer because of an open
identification — because one of them asserts nothing and the other asserts an existing obligation.

**PGR-23 (Unestablished — the residual's effect on the choice's size).** If the rationale is a ground, how large is
the report-only subset on which P-G1 would then retain one? Undrawn (CGE-28/29/30). So even in the branch where the
choice is a ground choice, its **magnitude is unmeasurable** — which means the branch does not restore a quantity
the owner could weigh, only a category difference.

**PGR-24 (Observed — no position is merged, dropped or recommended).** Finding that a distinction is conditional is
not a finding that a position should be removed. Dropping P-G0 or P-G1 from consideration would be a **selection**,
and this record makes none. Both remain available exactly as stated, and the choice between them remains the
owner's (PGR-31).

## 5. What P-G0's availability now rests on

**PGR-25 (Calculated — the dependency moved, and this is the reassessment's main result).** Before the decision,
P-G0's coherence turned on **DQ-1** (is D-3b's cause a ground?). The decision answers the cause half of DQ-1 **no**
(ODC-14), which removes that dependency. P-G0's coherence now turns on **two other questions**, neither answered
here and neither answered by ODC:

| # | Question | State | Why it decides P-G0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q-α** | does **D-3b reach lifecycle transitions**? | **candidate-level** (TGP-11; GRP-51) | if it reaches, "silence about the cause" is not a position but a violation of an existing decision (A-2) |
| **Q-β** | is the **reported rationale a ground**? | **open** (ODC-18) | if it is, P-G0's "no ground" must be read as excluding what P-G1's artifact reports, restoring GRP-21's structure |

**PGR-26 (Calculated — the ordering between them).** Q-α does not depend on Q-β and Q-β does not depend on Q-α.
Q-α is a **scope** question about an existing decision; Q-β is a **classification** question about content the
decision left open. Only Q-α can make P-G0 unavailable; Q-β can only make it a narrower claim. **This ordering is
analytic; it does not imply Q-α should be decided first, and this record does not say it should** (GRP-54's
discipline).

**PGR-27 (Calculated — DQ-1 is narrowed, not closed).** DQ-1 asked whether D-3b's cause is counted as a ground. Its
cause half is answered (no). Its **rationale half** — whether a report carried alongside the cause is a ground —
remains, and is now the whole of DQ-1. Recording this so DQ-1 is not read as discharged: a narrowed question that is
still open is still open.

**PGR-28 (Calculated — DQ-2's live set is unchanged in count).** GRP-54 held that DQ-1's answer "determines whether
DQ-2 has two live positions or three". Because the answer leaves P-G0 coherent (PGR-09/PGR-25), **DQ-2 still has
three live positions** — P-G0, P-G1, P-G2 — with P-G0's availability conditioned on Q-α rather than on DQ-1. No
position is removed from the set by this reassessment.

**PGR-29 (Observed — axis H is still the reason none of this orders them).** All three positions leave the validity
gap untouched (PGR-15; GRP-31). Nothing about the classification, the rationale's status, or D-3b's reach changes
the fact that no position supplies a condition under which a transition was admissible. ND-1 and the missing
predicate govern that, and neither is touched.

**PGR-30 (Observed — no requirement is created).** The reassessment adds nothing to what must be retained. It does
not require a rationale to be recorded, does not require it to be marked, does not widen D-3b, and does not convert
Q-α or Q-β into obligations. GRP-27 stands: P-G2's stream-wide obligation remains the only genuinely new requirement
in the option space, and it is unchanged (PGR-03, PGR-31).
