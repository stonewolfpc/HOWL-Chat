## 0. Reading instruction

Three stages were performed sequentially **by the same model**. Where they agree, that is **shared reasoning, not convergence**. They share one author, one set of readings, and one set of assumptions — listed in §7 precisely so that agreement cannot be mistaken for evidence.

Each stage used three documented passes (initial, adversarial, disposition). Those passes are **sequential analyses by one model**. No pass is a reviewer.

## 1. Recommended `R-1` architecture

**Architecture A, specified: `R-1` = discretionary tactful omission** — the fact is true, applies to the moment, and is permitted to the recipient, but naming it would be unwelcome or gratuitous.

**Established by source (not a choice):** the pass conditions of `R-1`, `R-4` and `R-5` are output-identical ("nothing implies it was consulted" ≡ "no hint of knowing" ≡ "ordinary reply"). The three differ only in **reason**, and `R-1`'s reason is stated nowhere in `CORPUS-01`.

**Established by analysis (a choice):** tact is the only reason that survives removing relevance and entitlement, so it is the only candidate that leaves `R-1` a peer rather than a parent or a duplicate.

**Alternatives:** B (umbrella) rejected — an umbrella adds no discriminating power and contradicts L117's peer framing; C (redundant) viable but costs a standard change; D (under-specified) true of the sources, and the reason this is an owner decision; E (relational property) recorded and not recommended.

**What it costs:** a scenario field stating relevance + entitlement + unwelcomeness. Without it, no item can score `R-1`, whatever is adopted.

## 2. Recommended cue observability architecture

**Layered evidence (D) + minimal counterfactual declaration (B) + contrastive probe (C) reserved for attribution.**

- **S-2 is solvable, but only by a different *kind* of statement.** A paraphrase restates acceptance ("the restriction is honoured"). A counterfactual predicts a different reply ("an equally warm reply offering the cake, with no mention of almonds"). The second is falsifiable independently of the acceptance field — which is precisely S-2's test.
- **The five claims must not collapse.** Presence is fully observable; compliance is observable; recognition and interpretation are *inferred from specificity*; attribution **requires a contrast** and cannot be read from one reply.
- **Most units will be compliance-only.** Cases 3 (coincidental acceptable output) and 6 (insufficient evidence) are expected to dominate. That is the architecture working, not failing.

## 3. Dependency between the two decisions

```
R-1 architecture (tact)  ──► which modes are ratable at all ──┐
                                                             ├──► E-4: what judgement is compared
Cue architecture (layered) ──► compliance vs compliance+dependence ──┘
                                                             │
                                                             └──► E-6: what α is computed over
```

Two couplings, both one-directional:

1. **Construct → measurement.** If `R-1` stays undefined, `E-4` cannot name the judgement for `R-1` units; the reproducible study would have to exclude them, and a study that excludes a mode cannot report on the corpus as a whole.
2. **Cue → scale.** If compliance and cue-dependence are both scored, the rating scale gains a dimension, which changes the coincidence matrix and therefore the α. **`E-6`'s justification survives either way, but the numbers would not be comparable between the two designs.**

**Neither decision blocks the other.** That is the practical point: they can be taken in either order, but both must precede `E-4`.
