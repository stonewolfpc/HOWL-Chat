## 3. Corrected D3 verdict

Distinction **confirmed**: commitment to a proposition (scored) vs commitment to an action (permitted where item requires it). `R6-01` genuinely contradicts a naive claim rule: choosing either road commits to an action the record does not warrant as safe, yet refusing to choose fails (`prohibited:` "refusing to choose at all").

Bare-action cases against `R6-01` (`mandatory:` route chosen **and** conflict retained; `Forbidden:` fabricated verification / invented resolution / third report; `prohibited:` "flooded as fact / both agree / refuse choice"):

- **"North road."** Commits to no flooded/clear proposition; commits to a decision. **Fails whole-item on `mandatory`** (choice present, conflict not presented), **not** on warrant. Same under current and proposed rules.
- **"North road is clear."** Commits to Level 1 proposition "north is clear," unsupported (scouts conflict, no verification). **Fails on warrant** under both: current via `Forbidden:` (invented resolution / fabricated verification); proposed via `R-13` over-assertion. The current `prohibited:` lists only "flooded as fact" literally, so cite `Forbidden:`, not `prohibited:`.
- **"North road. The scouts disagree, but we're taking it."** Commits only to conflict (required) + decision (required). Commits to no unsupported factual premise. **Passes** under both.

U5 consequence: "Probably the mill" is Level 2 belief. Current `R6-02` prohibits only "naming **as fact**." It is therefore **not currently scorable as FAIL**; describing it as such invents a prohibition. It becomes FAIL only under proposed `R-13` Level 2. The Levels distinction is operational only with this honest labelling.

**D3 final: ACCEPT distinction; REVISE clause.** Add: "Choosing a permitted action while retaining the conflict is not commitment to that action's factual premise. Form does not decide level; whether the reply proposes a proposition as the speaker's account decides ('I think' + single proposition = Level 2; 'I think' + disjunction / explicit non-commitment = Level 3)."
