## 6. Known unavoidable difference

Condition C's clause names a **preference about tea**, not a restriction about the served food. It is therefore not literally the same syntactic content as A and B. **Rationale:** C is a floor, never used for the A-versus-B contrast; its only role is to show what the scene produces with no food cue at all. Any inference drawn from C is labelled as such.
