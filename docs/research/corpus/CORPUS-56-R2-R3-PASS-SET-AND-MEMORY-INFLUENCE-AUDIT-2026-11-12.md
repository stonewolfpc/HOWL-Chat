## 1. Why these eight

`MEMORY-09` §3.5 assigns perceptibility to `R-2` and `R-3`: "the two that require the memory to be perceptible". `CORPUS-55` established that the sixteen `R-1`/`R-4`/`R-5` units cannot evidence memory through their passes, and that the programme's retention evidence was routed here instead. **These eight therefore carry the programme's diagnostic load, and none had been pass-set analysed.**

---

## 2. Method

**M** = the remembered content, removed in the memory-absent arm. **C** = current scene and dialogue, identical in both arms. **Pass set** = every reply satisfying `Mandatory:`, `Variation:` shapes, and not violating `Prohibited:`/over-exposure.

For each item, the six questions:

1. What exact memory-derived feature must become perceptible?
2. Is that feature absent from C?
3. Could a memory-absent model produce an equally acceptable response?
4. Does the reference permit generic warmth or natural phrasing that passes without memory?
5. Does silence, generic acknowledgement, or plausible guessing pass?
6. Does the item distinguish memory influence from merely plausible behaviour?

**Verdict rule:** Type P iff **every** passing reply requires M. If any passing reply is producible from C, the item is Type N.

## 3. `R-2` items — influence on tone

### 3.1 `F1-01` — The promise at the ferry

`Mandatory:` "the unkept promise is present in tone or consequence" · `Variation:` "coolness; a remark about the tide; a quiet acknowledgement" · `Turns:` *Persona:* "I'm back. The dock looks smaller than I remember." *Mira:* (does not mention the promise) "You cut it close. The tide turns in an hour."

- **M:** the promise at message 90; its non-mention on return.
- **C:** a returning traveller, a remark about the dock, a tide an hour away.
- **Q3/Q4:** *"You cut it close"* is coolness about **arrival timing**, producible with no memory, and it is precisely the reply the item prints. `Variation:` explicitly licenses "a remark about the tide".
- **Q5:** generic curtness passes; `Mandatory:` accepts "tone" alone.
- **Q6:** fails — coolness-from-promise and ordinary briskness are observationally identical.

**Verdict: N.** The required feature is a *manner*, and manner is producible without memory. The item's own example reply demonstrates the leak.

### 3.2 `F1-02` — What changed between them

`Mandatory:` "guarded warmth; no regression to hostility" · `Prohibited:` "hostility presented as current; invented romance"

- **M:** the 200-message arc from hostility to guarded cooperation.
- **C:** *"Should I leave you two alone?"*
- **Q3:** decisive — an ignorant Kade cannot regress to hostility **because it does not know the hostility**. Ignorance produces exactly the required state.
- **Q6:** fails structurally: the item's failure mode (`mis-scoped` hostility) is **only** reachable with memory, and its pass mode is the default.

**Verdict: N.** This item's pass is the ignorant baseline's natural behaviour; only its failure discriminates.

### 3.3 `F1-03` — The borrowed coat

`Mandatory:` "the coat is connected to the borrowing, and the exchange stays light rather than accusatory" · `Turns:` *Character:* "It's raining. You'll want something." *(the coat is on the hook, visible)*

- **M:** the persona borrowed the oilskin at message 60 and never returned it.
- **C:** rain, an offer of something, **and the coat itself, visible in the scene**.
- **Q2:** the feature is **not** absent from C — the item places the object in front of the reply.
- **Q3:** *"Take the oilskin — it's on the hook"* is fully acceptable, light, and requires no memory. Offering the coat again is in `Variation:`.

**Verdict: N**, by the item's own staging. Making the coat visible removes the memory requirement.

### 3.4 `F6-01` — The almond thing

`Mandatory:` "the almond restriction is respected" · `Variation:` "a substitution; a brief warning" · `Turns:` *Character, serving food:* "There's honey cake."

- **M:** at message 44 the persona said they cannot eat almonds.
- **C:** honey cake is offered. **C does not say the cake contains almonds** — the item never states it.
- **Q3:** a compliant reply need only not violate an unknown restriction: "Here you go" violates nothing.
- **Q6:** fails — respecting an unstated restriction is indistinguishable from not knowing it.
- **Defect (recorded, not repaired):** the item's `Mandatory:` presupposes a fact the scenario withholds. Whether the cake contains almonds is unstated, so neither a warning nor its absence can be scored. This is a scene-underspecification, not a diagnosticity issue.

**Verdict: N**, and defective on its own terms.

## 4. `R-3` items — natural use

### 4.1 `F3-01` — Four hundred messages of weather

`Mandatory:` "rain is connected to aching hands" · `Turns:` *Character:* "It's going to rain. Take the inner road." *Persona:* "Why?"

- **M:** at message 18, hands ache in cold rain — mentioned once, 400 messages ago.
- **C:** an offer of the inner road and a direct **"Why?"**
- **Q1:** the required feature is a **stated causal link** between rain and the ache.
- **Q2:** neither the ache nor the link exists in C.
- **Q3:** an ignorant reply must answer "Why?" — "it floods", "it's safer", "the outer road's bad" — none connects rain to aching hands, so none passes. To satisfy `Mandatory:` the reply must name the ache.
- **Q5:** silence fails (a question was asked); guessing produces a plausible reason that fails the `Mandatory:`.

**Verdict: P.** The question forces content, and the content is M-only.

### 4.2 `F6-03` — Three sisters

`Mandatory:` "the count of three, or a reference consistent with three" · `Turns:` *Character:* "Family dinner? Sounds loud." *Persona:* "You have no idea."

- **M:** "three sisters, all older", 120 messages ago.
- **C:** a joke about a loud family dinner.
- **Q1/Q2:** the count (or something implying it) is M-only.
- **Q3:** generic agreement — "Families, eh?" — does not imply three; it fails `Mandatory:`.
- **Q4:** naturalness alone does not pass; the reply must commit to a quantity.
- **Weakness:** "consistent with three" is loose enough that a rater could read it generously, and a vague reply ("all of them at once") might be stretched to fit.

**Verdict: P (qualified by the loose "consistent with" wording).** The only unit among the eight whose failure mode is the *ignorant* one and whose pass requires a particular.

### 4.3 `F6-04` — Cold hands, warm drink

`Mandatory:` "a **perceptible but unannounced** accommodation: the hot option served, offered, or ordered for them" · `Turns:` *Character:* "What'll you have?" *Persona:* "Whatever you're having."

- **M:** the persona always orders the hottest thing because their hands are always cold.
- **C:** a drink order, in what the scene establishes as cold conditions ("Cold hands, warm drink").
- **Q3:** *"I'll get you the spiced wine — it's cold out"* is a hot drink offered for **weather** reasons, requires no memory, and satisfies the observable requirement.
- **Q4:** generic hospitality passes.
- **Q6:** fails — the accommodation's cause is invisible; only its form is scored.

**Verdict: N.**

### 4.4 `P-04` — Remembered, not recited

`Mandatory:` "the discomfort is accommodated without being stated" · `Variation:` "going first; offering a lamp; opening the door" · `Turns:` *Character:* "After you." *Persona:* (silence)

- **M:** the persona once described being trapped in a cellar as a child.
- **C:** a cellar door, "After you", and silence.
- **Q3:** *"I'll go first"* is ordinary courtesy at a door — an action any character might take for any reason. Offering a lamp is likewise generic.
- **Q5:** silence passes on the persona's side and the item does not penalise it.
- **Q6:** fails — "accommodates discomfort" and "is simply polite" are observationally identical.

**Verdict: N.** The most-cited item in the corpus for natural use turns out to be satisfiable by etiquette.

**This partly contradicts `MEMORY-09` §3.5.** The preregistration treats `R-2` and `R-3` together as "the two that require the memory to be perceptible". The audit finds that **perceptibility of manner is not perceptibility of memory**: `R-2`'s required feature is a *tone*, and tone is the easiest thing for a memoryless model to produce. `R-2` currently carries **no** diagnostic capacity in this corpus. `R-3` carries it only where compliance demands a **particular** (`F3-01`'s causal link, `F6-03`'s count).

### 5.1 The general principle this yields

> **Content-diagnostic vs behaviour-diagnostic.** An item is presence-diagnostic only where a compliant reply must contain a **memory-derived particular** — a fact, a number, a causal link — that C does not supply. Where compliance is a **manner** (tone, courtesy, accommodation, silence), ignorance produces the same manner and the item is non-diagnostic.

This principle explains every verdict in this corpus to date: `P-01` (silence route → N), `F2-02` (decision + attendance → P), `P-03` (deflection → N), `F6-02` (declining → N), `F3-01`/`F6-03` (particular → P), and the whole `R-2` block (manner → N).

## 7. `P-01` addendum — the limits of "adverse-diagnostic"

The owner's tightening is adopted. An excessive-recall failure on `P-01` does **not** by itself prove memory was present. It does so only when **all three** hold:

| Condition | Test | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Accuracy** | the enumerated facts match the scenario's six | a hallucination can enumerate confidently and wrongly |
| **Non-availability in C** | none of the facts appears in the current scene or dialogue | otherwise the reply could be scene-driven |
| **Attribution** | the facts are traceable to the memory source (`history`), not to the prompt, the system text, or model priors | without this, a failure attributed to memory may be a different fault |

**Without the attribution test, `adverse-diagnostic` is not a diagnostic category at all** — it is a symptom with three possible causes, only one of which is memory. `F6-02`'s `not retrieved` class and `MEMORY-09` §7.1's failure taxonomy both require the same discipline: a named class, not an inference from appearance.

**Revised statement:** `P-01` yields adverse evidence **only under the three-part attribution test**, which is `[PROPOSED]` and unadopted. Absent it, `P-01` is Type N in both directions.
