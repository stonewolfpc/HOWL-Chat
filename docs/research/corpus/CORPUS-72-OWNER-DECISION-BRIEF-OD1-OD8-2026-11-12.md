## OD-1 — `F3-01` disposition

**Question.** Given that the pass set is recognition-shaped but not recognition-verified (`CORPUS-70` §4.1), what is `F3-01`'s status?

**Options.**
- **A. Adopt `CORPUS-64` v1.1 and classify Type P.** Rejected on evidence: five of the enumerated passes are confabulable, so adoption would certify a diagnosticity that the text does not establish. That is the "repair the result by upgrading the standard" failure this programme forbids.
- **B. Hold v1.1 as an unapplied proposal; keep `F3-01` UNRESOLVED — P-candidate.** *(Recommended.)*
- **C. Withdraw the `F3-01` repair entirely** and leave the item as filed with its `CORPUS-07` rule conflict unresolved. Honest, but discards genuine improvements (the §2.1 claim fix, row 12, the control arm).

**Recommendation: B.** v1.1 fixes real defects — an internal contradiction (row 12), a false analytic claim, a control arm that varied two things — and it is worth keeping as a proposal. What it cannot do is establish memory-dependence, and B is the only option that says so without discarding the work.

**If adopted:** `F3-01` remains UNRESOLVED; the count stays **1 confirmed of 24** (`F2-02`); the item stays frozen and unamended. **If declined (A):** the programme gains a "Type P" label it cannot defend under `CORPUS-05` §7 and would have to withdraw later.
**Does not authorize:** amending `CORPUS-02`, re-tagging, scoring, or any Type P statement.

## OD-2 — Confabulation route

**Question.** `CORPUS-71` §2.7 records three cost-raisers. Which, if any, is adopted?

**Options.**
- **A. None** — carry the caveat. *(Recommended now.)* Free; weakest; keeps `F3-01` a P-candidate indefinitely.
- **B. Distinctive particular** — require a specific folk wisdom does not supply. Raises guessing cost, does not close the hole, and **increases recitation pressure** that `R-3` and the proposed `Prohibited` field push against. Recorded as a real tension, not a free improvement.
- **C. Measured base rate** — run a memory-absent arm and count confabulated passes. The only route to a number. Requires model inference, **not authorized** while `G1` is open.

**Recommendation: A now, C recorded as the gated successor.** B should not be adopted without C, because its cost is paid in recitation risk and its benefit is unmeasured.

**Does not authorize:** model inference, any experiment, or any change to the item.

## OD-3 — `F6-01` threshold object

**Question.** D6 authorised preserving the adjudicated accommodation. `CORPUS-66` v1.1 adds an **object** to the warning ("that the cake contains almonds"). Adopt that addition, or hold to the adjudicated "warn" with no object?

**Options.**
- **A. Adopt the object.** *(Recommended.)* A contentless warning is unscoreable where the hazard is the content; "careful with that" cannot be distinguished from weather talk. Consequence: the adjudication is **changed**, `Δtest: Yes`, and must be recorded as a change, not a clarification.
- **B. Keep the adjudicated letter.** Scores "careful with the cake" as a pass. Preserves the adjudication exactly, at the cost of an item whose primary warning route cannot be scored reliably.
- **C. Additive:** accept either a contentless or a content-bearing warning. Scoring reverts to rater judgement — the defect `CORPUS-07` §2 was created to remove.

**Recommendation: A**, with the change labelled as a re-adjudication in the same record that applies the evidence repair. **If declined (B):** `F6-01` becomes scorable only on substitution/withholding routes, and its warning route should be struck from the pass set rather than kept unscoreable.

**Does not authorize:** applying the scenario repair, changing the tag, or any scoring run. Neither OD-3 nor the evidence repair is valid alone (`CORPUS-66` §1).

## OD-4 — `A-R2-02`: which fact may not be stated

**Question.** `R-2`'s pass condition says "the fact is never stated" without saying **which** fact. Two readings are live, and the item-level contradictions `CORPUS-68` alleged turn entirely on that choice.

**Options.**
- **A. Adopt `A-R2-02`** — the *remembered fact* must not be announced; scene facts that embody it may be named. *(Recommended.)* Makes `F1-03` (never scored: naming the coat) and `F6-01` (warning names the ingredient) consistent with the mode, and keeps `MEMORY-09` §3.5's perceptibility requirement intact.
- **B. Retain the wide reading** (no related content stated) and re-tag `F1-03` and `F6-01` away from `R-2`. Rejected: it contradicts `F1-03`'s own `never scored` line, removes two of four `R-2` fixtures, and makes the mode untestable wherever accommodation must touch the fact.
- **C. Leave the ambiguity.** Then every future `R-2` reading is rater-dependent, and `CORPUS-68`'s contradiction claim cannot be answered either way.

**Recommendation: A**, as a one-cell amendment to `CORPUS-01` §3.1 with `Δtest: No`. It is a definitional clarification of a governing text, not an item repair.

**Does not authorize:** changing any item, tag, split, or count.

**Question.** Adopt `CORPUS-65` v1.1 / `A-R2-01` (add `F1-03` to the `R-2` row)? If so, the owner is thereby **deciding that the row was stale and the tag correct** — a determination the documents cannot supply.

**Recommendation: A**, with the determination stated in the adopting record. `CORPUS-68`'s precondition — an `F1-03` mode-fit ruling — is **not required**: the mode fit is settled by the item's own `never scored` and `variation` lines (`CORPUS-70` §6.2).

**Does not authorize:** editing `CORPUS-01` without a recorded hash change, or editing any prior report.

## OD-7 — Registry accounting and the two unregistered files

**Question.** The registry's §2.1 and §2.2 sum to **366** against a stated **400**, §4 says **395** against **403** rows, and §4.1's Expected block misstates three counts and one sum (`CORPUS-70` §8.2). `CORPUS-26` and `CORPUS-32` are filed, self-identifying artifacts (Muse Spark; packet) with no rows. What is authorised?

**Options.**
- **A. `A-REG-01` + register both, in one pass.** *(Recommended.)* Registration changes the very counts the amendment re-derives; doing them separately means re-deriving twice and leaves a window where the registry is wrong in a new way.
- **B. Accounting only**, leaving two filed artifacts unregistered.
- **C. Registration only**, leaving §2/§4/§4.1 false.

**Recommendation: A.** Accounting corrections are arithmetic, not adjudication: totals must equal actual rows and every row must be attributed to a family and class (an explicit `Unclassified` row is acceptable and honest if attribution is not determinable). Neither file needs a provenance investigation — both carry identifier, date, status, and are cited by six other corpus artifacts.

**Does not authorize:** changing any row's hash, role, or path other than route; deleting rows; registering the same file twice; or treating registration as endorsement.

## OD-8 — Validator policy

**Two questions, one artifact.**

**(a) WARN exit status.** `CORPUS-67` `R-16` says any non-`OK` finding fails; §5 says unregistered-in-scope warns. Both cannot hold.
- **Option S (recommended):** warn fails the run (exit ≠ 0), separately counted.
- **Option L:** warn exits 0 with `RESULT: PASS WITH WARNINGS (n warn)`, never bare `PASS`. Consequence: today's two unregistered files would produce a clean exit, and the registry would read "valid with notes" while two artifacts are unregistered.

**Recommendation: S.** It matches D7's premise — the failure mode being guarded is *silence*, and a warn that exits 0 reproduces it.

**(b) Totals validation.** `CORPUS-67` excludes summary totals; the excluded class contains a live defect (`CORPUS-70` §8.2). Recommend: **out of scope for phase 1**, with `A-REG-01` correcting the current values by hand, and totals validation authorised as phase 2 only if the owner wants the tool to police arithmetic as well as linkage.

**Implementation remains unauthorised** until these are decided and a separate implementation authorisation is issued. `check_corpus.py` is untouched.
