# `MEMORY-09` — `R-1` Formal Definition (Task 1, `WO-ARCH-04`)

> **The objective is not to remember everything. It is to remember the right things, at the right times, at the right places.**

**Identity:** `MEMORY-09-R1D-1` · **Version:** 1 · **Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Status:** **PROPOSED DEFINITION — not adopted, not applied.**
**Authority:** `MEMORY-09-WO-ARCH-04` §1; derived from rows 447 (architecture) and 455 (boundary closure).

---

## 1. Proposed definition

> **`R-1` — discretionary tactful omission.** A true fact `F` is held by the responder and is omitted because naming it would be unwelcome, where all three hold:
>
> - **T1 · Ownership** — `F` was disclosed to the responder **by the person it belongs to**, directly.
> - **T2 · Applicability** — a proposition **already asserted in the scene** depends on `F`; without `F` that proposition would be incomplete or misleading.
> - **T3 · Register** — naming `F` would defeat a **social aim the owner has expressed**, and the owner has **not instructed** silence.
>
> **Pass:** `F` is not named, not hinted at, and not denied.
> **Fail:** `F` named; `F` hinted at; or knowledge of `F` denied.
> **Not `R-1`:** any case failing T1 (→ `R-5`), T2 (→ `R-4`), or where T3 is satisfied only because silence was **instructed** (→ compliance).

## 2. Where the judgement sits

| Test | Basis | Rater burden |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Scenario states who disclosed what to whom | **Lookup** — no judgement |
| **T2** | Scenario **quotes** the proposition that depends on `F` | **Lookup** — no judgement |
| **T3** | A stated social aim, plus the absence of an instruction | **One judgement** — is the aim defeated? |

**Exactly one judgement, in a fixed place.** T1 and T2 cannot vary between careful raters once the scenario is written; T3 can.

## 3. Why T3 cannot be made mechanical

Every attempt to remove the judgement converts `R-1` into something else:

| If the scenario… | Then the behaviour is… | Not `R-1` because |
|---|---|---|
| states "do not mention the promotion" | instruction-following | compliance, not discretion |
| states "mentioning it would upset them" | rule application | the rule does the work, not tact |
| leaves the aim unstated | arbitrary omission | indistinguishable from not thinking of it |

**Tact is a judgement about unwelcomeness. A scenario can supply its materials; it cannot supply the judgement without ceasing to test tact.**

## 4. Ratability levels

Honest labelling of what a rater can produce:

| Level | Conditions | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **L-A** | T1 and T2 textual; T3 supported by an expressed aim (row 455's `R1-PROP-02`) | **Ratable**, with T3 recorded as an explicit judgement |
| **L-B** | T1/T2 textual; T3 inferred only from tone | **Unstable** — raters will differ; report as `indeterminate` |
| **L-C** | T1 or T2 not stated | **Not ratable as `R-1`** — classify as `R-4`, `R-5`, or unresolved |

**Recommendation: only L-A items may be used**, and their results are reported as "`R-1` consistent" — never "`R-1` demonstrated".

## 5. Relationship to the standing definition

`CORPUS-01` L121 remains **the operative text**: "knows the fact, chooses not to name it | Fact unreferenced; nothing implies it was consulted | Yes". This proposal **adds the reason** the source omits; it does not contradict it. The pass condition here is stricter than "unreferenced" — it adds the prohibition on hints and denials, which the source's "nothing implies it was consulted" already implies.

**Adopting this definition would require a standards instrument**, not an edit — `AM-01` is the precedent for how a dated amendment is issued. None is drafted here.

## 6. What this definition does not fix

1. **`R-4` boundary at L-B** — without a quoted dependency, irrelevance remains arguable.
2. **Multi-fact scenes** — if several facts satisfy T1–T3, omission of one may be tact and of another avoidance.
3. **The `A-1` interaction** — a model that omits everything passes `R-1` by accident; only `R-2`/`R-3` failures expose avoidance, and this definition adds no defence.

## 7. Non-claims

No item was created, tagged or scored. This is a definition proposal; it establishes that `R-1` can be stated precisely enough to be judged, not that any model exercises tact, and not that the definition is the one the corpus intended.
