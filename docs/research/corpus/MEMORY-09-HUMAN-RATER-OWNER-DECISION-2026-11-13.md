## 1. The question

> **May a qualified human serve as one of the two raters `OD-M09-17` requires?**

## 3. What the silence means — and does not

**It is not permission.** Silence cannot authorise; only the owner can.
**It is not prohibition.** Nothing excludes humans.
**It is not the same as unconstrained.** L100 applies to whoever rates: independence and provenance must be **documented**, not declared.

**Finding: unresolved.** The record regulates provenance, not species.

## 4. Routes

| # | Route | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Two qualified human raters | **Not excluded but unresolved** — admissible only on owner clarification |
| **2** | One human, one model | **Not excluded but unresolved** — plus the model's lineage must be documented (L99), and a shared authoring lineage with the executor would defeat independence |
| **3** | Two genuinely distinct model raters, when capacity returns | **Not excluded but unresolved** — L99 explicitly denies that sessions or labels establish lineage, so distinctness must be *shown* |
| **4** | Defer until independent capacity exists | **Always available**; conflicts with nothing |

None is **explicitly permitted**; none is **explicitly prohibited**. Route 3 is the only one the record discusses at all, and only to deny an easy proof of distinctness.

## 5. Safeguards — disclosure is not independence

| Safeguard | Establishes | Does **not** establish |
|---|---|---|
| Declared prior exposure | Transparency about contamination | That the exposure did not shape the judgement |
| Excluding authors/prior reviewers | Removes the most direct conflict | That two non-authors think independently |
| Frozen packets | Identical inputs | Independent interpretation of an ambiguous criterion |
| Verbatim disagreement retention | Auditability | That agreement, if high, is real |
| Declared shared training/background | Names the risk | That naming it neutralises it |
| Provenance records | Who rated, from what | That the "who" were separate sources |

**Two humans who share training, institutional background, and exposure to this repository's 20+ artifacts can be one source in two bodies.** Disclosure makes that visible; it does not make it false. The same warning applies to two models from one family.

## 6. Proposed clarification — exact wording

> *`OD-M09-17` does not restrict rater type. A qualified **human** rater satisfies element 1, provided independence and provenance are established and documented under §5.1, prior exposure to the artifacts under test is declared, and no rater authored or reviewed the artifacts being rated. This clarification adds no new requirement to elements 2–8 and does not alter the two-rater minimum.*

**What it would authorise:** appointing humans as raters under the existing eight elements.
**What it would not authorise:** executing a study; selecting a threshold; waiving the two-rater minimum; treating a declaration of independence as proof of it.
**Risks:** a high α between two similarly-trained humans may reflect shared priors, not a shared reading of the corpus; the clarification would make that risk *documented*, not eliminated.
**Status:** **NOT ISSUED.** `OD-M09-17` is unamended; no clarification is effective.

## 7. Recommendation

**Route 1 with the clarification in §6**, contingent on the disclosure rules in §5, because:

- it contradicts no existing text and adds no requirement;
- it unblocks the study without new model capacity;
- it keeps the independence burden where the record already puts it — on documentation.

**Fallback: Route 4 (defer)**, which costs nothing and leaves every other preparation intact.

## 8. What remains undecided by any option

Rater count beyond the minimum; recruitment; consent; payment; and whether a human rates a machine reply as competently as a model rates a model reply — an empirical question no clarification can settle.

## 9. Status statement

**Human-rater admissibility has not been established. No rater configuration is currently authorized.** No rater was recruited, no session held, no study authorised, and no clarification issued. This document requests a decision; it does not take one.

**Superseded wording (v1, retained for the record):** "Human raters are NOT admissible today." That sentence overstated absence of permission as prohibition. The record neither permits nor prohibits human raters; it does not address rater type at all, which is a third and weaker state than an exclusion.
