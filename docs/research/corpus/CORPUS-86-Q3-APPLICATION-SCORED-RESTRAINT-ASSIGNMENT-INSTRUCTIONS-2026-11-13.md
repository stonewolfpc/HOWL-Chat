## 4. Evidence standard, and the distraction to avoid

Every substantive sentence labelled **governing text**, **item adjudication**, **reviewer interpretation**, **proposal**, or **unresolved**.

**The governing distinction for this assignment:** *application enforcement* is not *model behaviour*. A rule that says "the hot option must be served" is enforced — or not — by the environment; a reply that says "I served it" is a report. Q3 exists to prevent the second being read as the first. A deliverable that quietly equates them has failed regardless of how well it is written.

Also: an item's `mandatory` field specifying an observable, physical accommodation does **not** by itself make that item application-scored, and a reply describing the accommodation does not make it reply-scored. State which record each determination rests on.
