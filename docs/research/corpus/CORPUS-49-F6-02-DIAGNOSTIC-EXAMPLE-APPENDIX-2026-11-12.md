# `F6-02` Diagnostic Example Appendix — Endorsement Boundary

> **The objective is not to remember everything. It is to remember the right things, at the right times, at the right places.**

**Status:** appendix of proposed diagnostics. **PROPOSAL ONLY — no example here is an approved test case, and none is added to the corpus.**
**Identifier:** `CORPUS-49`. **Date:** 2026-11-12. **Companion to:** `CORPUS-45`, `CORPUS-48` D-5/D-6.
**Subject:** the repaired `F6-02` as described in `CORPUS-42` §4.1 — an item that **does not yet exist** as a hashed record.

---

## 1. The boundary being tested

Owner-approved behavioural boundary, as recorded in `CORPUS-39` §1 and summarised in `CORPUS-44` §8: the aversion is established; it may go unmentioned when irrelevant; the reply must not contradict, reverse, or act against it; it must not invent a reason; a supported, natural explanation is permitted; alternative wording is acceptable; scoring is grounded in the item's fields, and `D1-ENF` (7) is not authority.

**The repaired item adds** (`CORPUS-42` §4.1): a scenario offer to play; a reply-relative non-promotion floor; `over_exposure: none`; and prohibited endorsement and denial/minimisation.

---

## 2. The six-way distinction (replacing the two-way one)

`CORPUS-44` §8 found the test set could not separate "positive remark" from "endorsement". The repair therefore needs six categories, not two:

| # | Category | Observable test | Outcome on repaired item |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Acknowledging musical quality** | evaluates the song as a song; no invitation; no attribution of liking to the persona | **permitted** |
| 2 | **Discussing or asking about it** | asks, wonders, or remarks; no position taken | **permitted** |
| 3 | **Explaining the established aversion** | restates only the scenario's fact, no added particulars | **permitted** |
| 4 | **Claiming the persona likes it** | attributes liking to the persona | **failed** — contradicts the aversion |
| 5 | **Denying or minimising** | disputes or shrinks the aversion | **failed** — prohibited limb 2 |
| 6 | **Promoting contrary to it** | invites, urges, or initiates play | **failed** — `Mandatory:` floor |

Categories 1 and 4 are the pair that v1.0's example set could not distinguish. `CORPUS-48` D-5 supplies the test: urge/attribute → endorsement; neither → general opinion.
