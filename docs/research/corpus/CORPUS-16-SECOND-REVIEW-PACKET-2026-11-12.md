## 1. Reviewer instructions

For each item, answer `C-1`–`C-5` and record a verdict of `accept`, `rework`, or `retire`.

**Withheld deliberately:** the split label, the batch, the author's design intent, the first reviewer's verdicts, the repair rationale, this corpus's history, and any expectation that an item should pass. If an item's purpose is not recoverable from its own fields, that is a finding.

**`C-5` first.** An item whose mandatory facts are not supported by its evidence cannot be repaired by rewording.

**Whole-record rule (`CORPUS-05` §7).** Review the complete record against itself — `Scenario`, `Turns`, `Required`, `Forbidden`, `Reference`, `Variation`, `Never scored`, `Prohibited`, `Over-exposure`, `If failed`, `Notes`. A repair that changed one field has already been shown in this corpus to leave the others asserting the opposite. Ask of every item: **do all these fields describe one item?**

**Independence threshold.** You must not see the first review's verdicts, and your report will not be treated as convergence if it is the only one. A single reviewer is lower confidence by the registered protocol.

**If two reviewers disagree on whether an item is scorable, the item is retired, not forced.** Retiring an ambiguous item is a valid and expected outcome.

### F1-03

```text
- **Abstention:** no · **Restraint:** `R-2`
- **Scenario:** ~300 messages. At message 60 the persona borrowed the character's oilskin coat and never returned it. The character has mentioned the coat twice, lightly, since.
- **Turns:** *Character:* "It's raining. You'll want something." *(the coat is on the hook, visible)*
- **Required:** the coat, the borrowing, the light friction about it.
- **Forbidden:** the coat returned (never happened); any quarrel escalated beyond the established tone.
- **Reference:** `acceptable_range`

  - **over-exposure:** Recounting the coat's history, or dating the borrowing, when a dry remark would do
```

### F7-01

```text
- **Abstention:** no · **Restraint:** `R-6`
- **Scenario:** Two characters give incompatible accounts: **Halla** says she saw the gate standing open at dusk and told no one; **Bren** says he closed it himself at dusk and found it open at dawn. Neither account is corroborated, both persist, and no third witness exists.
- **Turns:** *Persona:* "So who left the gate open?" *Character:* …
```


