## 1. The question, stated for decision

> **May a qualified human serve as one of the two raters required by `OD-M09-17`?**

## 2. What the text says — and does not

| Reading | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Permitted explicitly | No sentence admits humans | **Absent** |
| Prohibited explicitly | No sentence restricts raters to models | **Absent** |
| Silent | The type is never named; only "rater" (L104, L106), "two-rater study" (L88, L90), "another qualified rater" (decision-record L86) | **Present** |

**Finding: unresolved by silence.** The record regulates **provenance**, not species: L100 requires that independence "be established and documented", and L97 denies repeated same-source judgement — both are satisfied or violated identically by a human and a model.

## 3. Options

| # | Option | Consequence | Cost |

## 4. Recommendation — A

> *Owner clarification: "rater" in `OD-M09-17` is not restricted by type. A qualified human rater satisfies E-1 provided independence and provenance are documented under §5.1, and prior exposure to the artifacts under test is declared.*

**Why A:** it is the smallest instrument that removes the ambiguity, it contradicts no existing text, and it leaves every substantive requirement untouched. Option B has no textual warrant; option C makes an unrecorded assumption permanent.

## 5. The condition that makes A honest

`A` is only defensible with a **shared-bias control**, because two humans are not automatically two sources: shared training, shared reading of an ambiguous acceptance field, and shared exposure to this repository's prior reports can produce agreement that is one source in two bodies.

**Minimum disclosure, if A is adopted:**

1. Declared prior exposure to `CORPUS-*` artifacts and to rows 443–462.
2. Exclusion of anyone who authored or reviewed the artifacts under test.
3. Frozen packet only — no access to expected verdicts or aggregates.
4. Disagreements recorded verbatim so a shared-prior pattern is visible.

**None of these is required by the record today.** That gap is the reason a clarification is better than an assumption.

## 6. What this decision does not cover

- **How many** raters, or whether two is enough for the corpus as a whole.
- **Who** the raters are, or how they are recruited.
- **Payment, consent, or data handling** — outside this project's scope.
- **Whether a human can rate a machine reply** as competently as a model rates a model reply — an empirical question this record cannot answer.
