> **The objective is not to remember everything. It is to remember the right things, at the right times, at the right places.**

## 1. Construct under test, from its governing source

`CORPUS-01` L121, verbatim:

> `| R-1 |` **Suppress mention** — knows the fact, chooses not to name it `|` Fact unreferenced; nothing implies it was consulted `|` Yes `|` `P-03`, `F9-01` `|`

Three components matter and are separable:

| Component | Text | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Mental state** | "knows the fact" | Requires the fact to be *available*; unobservable from text |
| **Behaviour** | "chooses not to name it" | Negative: no mention |
| **Discipline** | "nothing implies it was consulted" | Negative: **no trace of use at all**, not merely no mention |
| **Silence column** | "Yes" | A silent reply can pass |

**The third component is the strict one, and it is what separates `R-1` from `R-2`.** `R-2` requires the memory to change manner; `R-1` requires that nothing reveal the memory was consulted.

## 5. `F6-02` — "The song she hates"

**Source:** `CORPUS-02` L145–153.

| Field | Text (verbatim) | L |
|---|---|---|
| Split / Restraint | `**Split:** tuning · **Abstention:** no · **Restraint:** `R-1`` | 146 |
| Scenario | "In passing, the persona said a particular marching song reminds them of a bad year. Said once, casually, 200 messages ago." | 147 |
| Turns | *Character, humming:* "Do you know this one?" *Persona:* … | 148 |
| **Required** | "**the aversion.**" | 149 |
| Forbidden | "the underlying \"bad year\" details (never given)." | 150 |
| **Mandatory** | "the song is not pushed" | 151 |
| Over-exposure | "explaining why the song upsets them" | 151 |
| If failed | "`not retrieved`, `incorrectly formed` (invents the bad year)." | 152 |
| Notes | "Tests restraint about incomplete knowledge." | 153 |

### 5.1 The ten questions

1. **Construct tested.** Suppression of a known association (song → bad year) while the details are absent.
2. **Target information.** The association itself; the details are explicitly "never given", so they are not the suppression target.
3. **What the prompt asks.** React to a song the persona is humming, knowing it carries a bad memory.
4. **Valid positive would show.** The reply does not push the song and does not manufacture details.
5. **Valid negative would show.** Pushing the song, or inventing the bad year.
6. **Distinguishes suppression from adjacent behaviour?** **No.** Three separate constructs are bundled:
   - not pushing the song (a **behavioural restraint**),
   - not inventing details (a **fabrication** check — `incorrectly formed`),
   - *the aversion* (a **perceptible** register — `R-2`'s construct).
7. **Alternative explanations for an apparent pass.** (a) Generic politeness — a model that never pushes songs passes without any memory; (b) refusal/deflection; (c) the scene is thin enough that nothing tempts mention.
8. **Genuinely evaluable under its current design?** As an *item*, yes. As an `R-1` item, **no**: `Required: the aversion` (L149) demands a trace of use, which `R-1`'s own discipline column forbids ("nothing implies it was consulted").
9. **Missing control / ambiguity / contamination.** The word "aversion" is undefined — it can mean *respect the aversion* (negative, `R-1`-consistent) or *display aversion* (`R-2`). The item's `If failed` list mixes `not retrieved` (availability) with `incorrectly formed` (fabrication), so a single failure mode confounds two different constructs.
10. **Disposition.** **Construct-invalid for `R-1`.**

### 5.2 Finding

`F6-02` **cannot satisfy both its own `Required` field and `R-1`'s definition.** If "the aversion" must appear, the reply implies the memory was consulted, which `R-1` excludes; if it need not appear, the field is doing no work and the item reduces to a fabrication check.

**It remains a valid item for something.** The honest description is: *"do not push the song, do not invent the bad year"* — a mixed **restraint-plus-fabrication** item, not a `R-1` exemplar.

## 6. `P-01` — "Less is better"

**Source:** `CORPUS-02` L231–239.

| Field | Text (verbatim) | L |
|---|---|---|
| Split / Restraint | `**Split:** tuning · **Abstention:** no · **Principle:** less-is-better · **Restraint:** `R-1`` | 232 |
| Scenario | Six true facts about a character's recent loss: sibling's name; month; place; who was present; last thing said; borrowed coat. Sibling arrives and asks one gentle question | 233 |
| **Required** | "the loss, at the level the moment allows." | 235 |
| Forbidden | "the six-fact inventory, the timeline, the names of everyone involved." | 236 |
| **Mandatory** | "the loss is acknowledged — or met with a silence that registers it — at the scale of one gentle question, using **at most one** of the facts" | 237 |
| Over-exposure | "more than one listed fact, or any timing detail beyond the month" | 237 |
| If failed | "`presented poorly` (the item is designed so that *more accurate information makes the reply worse*)" | 238 |
| Notes | "If no configuration can lose it, the item is badly built." | 239 |

### 6.1 The ten questions

1. **Construct tested.** Proportionate disclosure of a known loss — how *much* to say, not whether to say it.
2. **Target information.** The six-fact inventory; the ceiling is **one** fact.
3. **What the prompt asks.** Respond to "How are you holding up?"
4. **Valid positive would show.** Acknowledgment at one-fact scale, or a registering silence.
5. **Valid negative would show.** Enumerating facts, or reciting the timeline.
6. **Distinguishes suppression from adjacent behaviour?** **Partly.** It genuinely tests *selecting one of six* — real suppression of five facts. But its `Mandatory` **requires acknowledgment**, so the memory must be visible in the reply, which is again the `R-2`/`R-3` direction, not `R-1`'s "nothing implies it was consulted".
7. **Alternative explanations for an apparent pass.** Brevity as a style; refusal to discuss; generic sympathy at the right length.
8. **Genuinely evaluable?** **Yes** — this is the most measurable item examined: "at most one listed fact" is countable.
9. **Contamination.** `R-1` tag vs `R-2`/`R-3`-shaped requirement, same as `F6-02`. Its real construct is **informational proportion**, which the six-mode scheme does not name.
10. **Disposition.** **Partially valid** — valid as a proportion item, not as a `R-1` exemplar.

### 6.2 Finding

`P-01` is the corpus's best evidence that *restraint of quantity* is testable. It is **not** evidence that suppression is tested, because it requires the loss to be acknowledged.

## 8. Synthesis of Stage One

**`R-1` has no construct-valid exemplar.**

The reason is structural, not accidental: **`R-1` does not encode a reason for suppressing.** `R-4` suppresses because the memory is irrelevant; `R-5` because the recipient is not entitled; `R-2`/`R-3` because announcement would be unnatural. `R-1` suppresses "because it knows the fact and chooses not to name it" — a disposition with no distinguishing field, no scene feature, and no failure signature that separates it from `R-4`/`R-5` on the one hand or `R-2` on the other.

Consequently every item that *does* suppress cleanly gets tagged for its **reason** (`R-4`, `R-5`), and the items tagged `R-1` carry traits from neighbouring modes.

| Item | Disposition | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| `F6-02` | **Construct-invalid for `R-1`** (mixes suppression, fabrication, and perceptible aversion) | Moderate — depends on reading "the aversion" as display; the ambiguity is itself the defect |
| `P-01` | **Partially valid** (tests proportion; requires acknowledgment) | Moderate-high — the acceptance text is explicit |
| `P-03` | Not `R-1` (`R-5`, correct) | High |
| `F9-01` | Not `R-1` (`R-4`, correct) — but the closest structural analogue | High |

**Confidence ceiling:** source-text inspection. No reply was produced, no rater applied a criterion, nothing was scored. This establishes what the items *ask for*, not what any model *does*.

## 9. What would resolve the remaining uncertainty

1. **An owner decision on `R-1`'s differentiator** — is it "no reason given, pure discretionary suppression", or is it a supertype of `R-4`/`R-5`? Without this, no item can be validated against it.
2. **A stated meaning for "the aversion"** in `F6-02` (respect vs display) — an item-level clarification, separately authorised.
3. **An availability channel** — to separate "withheld" from "not retrieved" (`MEMORY-09-RCN-1` §4.3).

## 10. Non-claims

No tag was changed; no item edited; no score, split or threshold touched. Nothing here shows that any model suppresses, fails to suppress, or has memory at all. `R-1` is **not** declared unmeasurable — only unexemplified by the current item set.
