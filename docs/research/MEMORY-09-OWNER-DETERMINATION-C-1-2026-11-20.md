## 2. What is determined

**C1D-04 (Source-derived — the referent).** In §11's "world fact — **authority or corroboration**" (`:613`),
**authority** names the **epistemic standing of the claim's support** — the authoritative standing of its source —
and not a permission held by an actor to cause the transition.

**C1D-05 (Source-derived — the operation).** The clause belongs to **record-promotion**: a memory record leaving
`provisional` for `active` under a per-kind condition (CPA-05…CPA-07). It does not describe **authority-promotion**,
the v9 operation that creates a separate world/scenario record and settles which record governs truth (O-11; v9 rules
17–21).

**C1D-06 (Calculated — the two alternatives in the clause are both support conditions).** "Authority or
corroboration" offers an authoritative source **or** independent corroboration. Both are warrants for the claim; the
determination reads the first as the source-standing alternative, not as an actor's permission.

**C1D-07 (Source-derived — the scope is §11).** The determination is confined to §11's promotion condition. It is not
a finding that every occurrence of "authority" in r33 means the same thing, and it does not read the term into v9 or
the authority framework (CPA-34).

**C1D-08 (Observed — the character of the determination).** It is an **interpretation of intended purpose from the
surviving design evidence**, not proof of the original author's private intent. The audit's three-register separation
stands: intent is **Inferred**, the text's content is **Observed/Source-derived**, and later records' readings are
**Inferred** (CPA-41/42).

**C1D-09 (Calculated — why it is not a new requirement).** The determination says what the clause's word **means**,
not that a record must satisfy a new condition. It adds no obligation to the map, no evaluation point and no
consequence for non-satisfaction. It is a reading of existing text, not a rule over it.

## 4. What stays Unestablished

**C1D-13 (Observed — the four, preserved exactly).**

| # | Unestablished | Why it survives the determination |
|---|---|---|
| **U-1** | the **precise sufficiency standard** — what qualifies as an authoritative source, and what counts as sufficient corroboration | the determination fixes the **referent**, not the threshold; §11 names the alternatives and stops (CPA-22) |
| **U-2** | **permission to perform record-promotion** | the determination says the clause is not that permission; it does not say whether such a permission exists, who holds it, or where it is checked (CPA-28) |
| **U-3** | whether **authority-promotion can satisfy** the record-promotion condition | the seam ODBC-20 names: a valid authority-promotion does not automatically establish the separate epistemic condition (CPA-40) |
| **U-4** | whether **§11 is operative or descriptive** | the paragraph is untagged; the determination reads it **as written**, not as a finding that it governs (C1R-05/06) |

**C1D-14 (Calculated — U-1 is not a gap the determination could close).** Identifying "authority" as a warrant does
not supply a test for warrant. The threshold is a separate question, and the architecture already distinguishes
recording a typed basis from judging whether it is sufficient (O-8a-2; CPA-38).

**C1D-15 (Calculated — U-2 is not answered by elimination).** Because the clause is not a permission, the
determination removes one candidate owner for record-promotion permission; it does not install another. The earlier
"Narrowed" phrasing stands: authority-promotion is owned by O-8a; record-promotion's permission is not addressed
there (C1D-19; CPA-09).

**C1D-16 (Calculated — U-3 is a composition question, not a referent question).** An actor may hold authority to
create an authoritative world record without that act establishing that the resulting record satisfies §11's
epistemic condition. The two operations may compose in a pipeline; whether they do is not stated.

**C1D-17 (Calculated — U-4 keeps the determination conditional in force).** The determination is an interpretation
of the clause's meaning **if read as operative**. It does not assert that §11 has been adopted, and it does not
settle §8.1's competing entry value (`:404`; ECI-05…ECI-09).

**C1D-18 (Unestablished — the author's private intent).** The determination is an interpretation of **intended
purpose from surviving design evidence**, not proof of what the original author privately meant. §11's promotion
prose has a single witness in the corpus (CLV-09), and no author statement exists.
