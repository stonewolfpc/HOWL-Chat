## 2. The premise, and what it forecloses

**PGA-06 (Source-derived).** A statement-less **lifecycle** event must retain its actual cause (QAD-05). The
obligation is per-event, not per-stream (TGP-11; QAD-19): it binds the subset that lacks a source statement, and the
boundary of that subset is undrawn.

**PGA-07 (Calculated — the first foreclosure).** A position that retained **no cause** for statement-less lifecycle
events would now be **non-compliant**, not merely silent. A-2 applied to a class whose reach is no longer in doubt.

**PGA-08 (Calculated — the second foreclosure).** P-G1's content is now **the baseline**. Because D-3b coexists with
every position (A-1), "cause on statement-less transitions" is what happens whatever is chosen. Selecting P-G1 would
therefore **change nothing** — it names an obligation that already binds. QAX-29's conditional resolves: P-G1 is a
**description**, not a proposal, and it carries no authority of its own (QAD-20).

**PGA-09 (Calculated — P-G1 is the null decision).** Not "a cheap option" and not "a minimal policy", but the
**do-nothing** setting: the state of the world with D-3b applied and nothing further decided. That is a fact about
the position's content, not a judgment about whether it is the right choice.

**PGA-10 (Calculated — so the comparison is no longer between two retention policies).** One of the two positions
retains nothing of its own; the other is the baseline. The question is what, if anything, surrounds the baseline.

**PGA-11 (Observed — M-2 and M-3 are not positions and never were).** With both streams in reach they now bind
lifecycle events as well, but they remain **constraints on production** (GRP-24): they are satisfied or violated
identically under P-G0, P-G1 and P-G2, and they discriminate nothing.

**PGA-12 (Calculated — the statement-bearing region is still open).** GRP-26's region — a transition that carries a
source statement — is untouched by Q-α. D-3b does not reach it, no provision requires a basis for it, and its size is
unestablished. **This is now the only region in which any position can differ at write time.**

not sit on one scale. Post-Q-α the coverage question is closed on the statement-less side (PGA-06) and unchanged on

| Setting | Position | Obligation it adds | Provenance |

**PGA-31 (Narrowed — GRP-20 and GRP-29 are superseded in part).** Their "silence, decided floor, undecided addition"
characterisation was correct while the cause's reach was open. It is now: **a prohibition, a baseline, and an

**PGA-32 (Calculated — and the axis is permissiveness, not coverage).** All three settings retain the **same** thing
from D-3b (PGA-13). They differ in what they **permit or require beyond it**. So the choice was never "how much do we
keep" — after Q-α, it is "what do we forbid, allow, or demand on top of what already binds".

**PGA-33 (Calculated — three settings on the prohibition reading, two on the label reading).** If P-G0 is P-G0-a, the
axis has two settings (ungoverned, require) and P-G0 is a naming claim, not a setting. **Which it is depends on
PGA-17's undrawn line, and on nothing else.**

**PGA-34 (Observed — axis H is still identical).** No setting supplies a validity basis: a prohibition records
nothing, silence records nothing, a requirement records **claims** (GRP-31/GRP-35). ND-1 and the missing predicate

**PGA-35 (Observed — Q-α creates no authority).** Recording a cause authorises nothing and justifies nothing (QAD-12,
