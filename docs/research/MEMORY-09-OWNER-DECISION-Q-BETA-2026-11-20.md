## 2. What is decided

**QBD-05 (Source-derived).** **The actor-reported rationale is a ground** — and specifically **the ground of the
transition whose cause it reports.** Not a ground in general, not a ground of the stream, not a free-standing basis:
the ground of that transition, identified by the transition it belongs to.

**QBD-06 (Source-derived — the double reading is now threefold).** The artifact already carried two recognised
readings (ODC-05, ODC-06): a **cause** (what produced the event) and a **report of the actor's stated rationale** (what
the actor says motivated their action). Under this decision the same artifact also carries a **ground** (what basis was
claimed for the change — GRP-09; TGP-02). The readings are additional to one another; none displaces another.

**QBD-07 (Source-derived — confined to the report-only case).** The classification applies **only** where the only
access to the cause is the actor's account — an operator's stated intention, a narrator grant's purpose, a model's own
decision rationale (CGE-24). **Observable causes remain outside the decision.** A clock expiry's cause "is not a claim
about why the change was warranted; it is a fact about elapsed time" (CGE-26), and it is not classified as a ground
here.

**QBD-08 (Calculated — the cause is still not the ground).** ODC-14 stands: the cause is **not** a ground under
another name (CGE-20's R-1). What this decision adds is a third reading **co-located in the same artifact**, not an
identity. The cause remains the producer; the rationale remains the actor's statement; the ground is that statement
considered as the basis claimed for the change. Three questions, one record, no merger.

**QBD-09 (Source-derived — classification only).** No new requirement, field, marker or carrier. The decision states
what the retained content **is** in the report-only case; it does not require that a rationale be recorded, marked,
separated, tagged, assessed or checked, and it adds nothing to what must be retained (T-3).

**QBD-10 (Source-derived — no authority).** The classification grants no authority. **ND-1** — who may cause a
lifecycle change — remains **OPEN**, and **validity** remains open: the reported rationale is "a claim, not proof that
the transition was valid or warranted" (T-4). I17 governs unchanged: "A result earns authority from the conditions
under which it was established, not from when or where it was written" (r33 `:305`, `[X]`).

**QBD-11 (Source-derived — no prohibition).** No prohibition is created **or implied**. P-G0's permissive-by-omission
status (PGM-29) is untouched; nothing here forbids retaining a ground, and nothing here forbids declining one. Whether
a prohibition should exist remains a separate decision requiring its own record (PGM-30; T-5).

## 3. How this sits against the earlier decisions

**QBD-12 (Calculated — ODC-18 is the question this answers, and it was left open deliberately).** ODC-18 recorded two
readings of whether the actor-reported rationale is a ground, marked both "**not chosen**", and stated that recording
the residual question "is not an invitation to answer it". The second reading there was: "the rationale is a ground of
the weakest kind — P-G0 becomes a claim about vocabulary (GRP-21), as before, though now confined by ODC-07". The
decision adopts that reading. It is the resolution of a named residual, not a revision of a settled classification.

**QBD-13 (Calculated — ODC-13's "(b)" is not what was chosen, and the distinction is recorded rather than glossed).**
CGE-43's (b) was "a report-only cause **is** a ground — which resolves it in favour of conditional equivalence and
makes D-3b a partial ground obligation"; ODC-13 answered "**not (b)** — the owner did not classify the cause as a
ground". Two differences survive and both matter:

| | CGE-43's (b) | **Q-β = A** |
|---|---|---|
| What is classified | the **cause** | the **rationale** the cause carries |
| What it does to D-3b | makes it a **partial ground obligation** | **no obligation changes** (T-3); only the description of what the retained cause is |
| Cause's identity | merged with the ground | **distinct** (ODC-14; QBD-08) |

So ODC-13 is not reopened and ODC-14 is not reversed: the cause is not a ground, and D-3b is not converted into a
ground requirement. What changed is the status of the **report** that ODC-06 recognised alongside the cause.

**QBD-14 (Calculated — descriptively, the baseline does retain a ground; normatively nothing is added).** On this
answer, wherever the D-3b cause is known only from the actor's account, the retained artifact is a ground — so "the
baseline retains no ground" no longer holds over that subset (QBR-21, first row). That is a statement about **what the
existing retained content is**, not about what must be retained: the obligation remains M-1 as written (QAD-05),
applied as written, with no clause added.

**QBD-15 (Observed — ODC-07 continues to apply, undiminished).** "The reported rationale does not establish truth,
authority, or validity" (ODC-07) is not displaced by the classification. A report classified as a ground is still a
report; the label changes the vocabulary and the constraint that is said to attach, not the artifact's standing
(CGE-38).

**QBD-16 (Calculated — which constraint is now said to attach, stated with its label).** Under A, the constraint that
attaches to the ground reading is CLV-28 — "referenced by the transition, never read as the status". CLV-28 is an
**inference**, not canon (QBR-22), and it is not promoted by this decision. ODC-07 remains the owner-stated boundary
covering the same content. The two say the same thing in practice: what is recorded is a claim, never a standing.

**QBD-17 (Calculated — the description of P-G0 changes; no position is selected, ranked or removed).** Over the
report-only subset, P-G0's name — "no ground" — is no longer accurate as a description of what the baseline retains
(QBR-21). That is a consequence for **how the position would be described**, not a selection, not a ranking, and not a
removal of any position from DQ-2's live set. What it can no longer claim is that the present arrangement retains no
ground; and it is **not** a naming correction — choosing P-G0 would require displacing the artifact D-3b requires
(GRP-21/CGE-35; QAD-18). This record neither removes P-G0 from consideration nor confirms its availability: that is the
owner's retention decision (QBD-24).

**QBD-18 (Calculated — DQ-2's answer, if given, is now phrased differently).** Under B, "no ground requirement at
all"; under A, "no ground requirement beyond the one already present" (QBR-21). This is a difference in **how that
answer would read**, not an answer to DQ-2. Nothing in this record says whether a stream-wide ground requirement should
exist, and P-G2's obligation remains without textual basis (GRP-27).
