## 2. The pivot: what "authority" in §11 refers to

**NAB-05 (Source-derived — the text and its markers).** §11 (`:600`) gives the lifecycle diagram and then:

> "Promotion per kind: episodic — on witnessing; preference — immediate for holder; belief — never promotable to
> fact; rumour — stays provisional, repetition is not corroboration; world fact — authority or corroboration;
> absence — attributed." (`:611`–`:614`)

The §11 heading and this paragraph carry **no adoption marker**. §11.1 is "proposed, not adopted **[P]**" (`:619`)
and §11.3 likewise (`:687`); the document's legend defines only **[X]** (owner decision, authoritative design input)
and **[P]** (proposed, awaiting owner) (`:42`). §8.1's formation step — which writes `status=active` (`:404`) — is
also untagged. **What an untagged paragraph's adoption status is, is not stated** (NAB-09).

**NAB-06 (Source-derived — the phrase's grammar).** "Authority **or** corroboration" offers two alternatives for the
same promotion. Corroboration is an **evidential** relation (independent support for a claim). A term paired with it
as an alternative is most naturally read as another **warrant** — the standing of the source — rather than as a
permission. That is a reading of the sentence, not a definition (NAB-09).

**NAB-07 (Observed — how r33 uses the noun "authority").** Every occurrence of the noun is source-of-truth or
epistemic standing; none is a permission:

**NAB-08 (Observed — where r33 expresses permission, it does not use the noun).** Permission appears as

## 3. Q1 — can fold semantics be specified independently of the authority that permits entry?

**NAB-14 (Source-derived — the write/fold split).** K-R17 makes validity a **write-time** condition (`:117`,
`:137`): an unsupportable `revoked` is "rejected at write, not silently ignored". K-R14 then rebuilds each stream
from its events (`:113`), and `:366` folds events and compares the result to the stored projection. The pattern is:
**admissibility governs what may be appended; the fold governs what an appended log means.** LCM-19/LCM-21 record the
same split.

**NAB-15 (Calculated — three different things are being called "authority" at the entry point).**

| Thing | What it constrains | Where it acts |
|---|---|---|
| **permission** | whether an act may append an event | write gate |
| **warrant / promotion condition** | whether a state is justified for this record | formation or fold, depending on architecture (NAB-16) |
| **stream authority** | which artifact is the source of truth | architecture, not a decision |

A fold is a function over a log. Permission is not an input to that function; it decides whether a line exists. So
fold semantics is **independent of permission** in every architecture, and independent of the **warrant** only if the
warrant is applied before the fold's domain begins.

**NAB-16 (Calculated — three entry architectures, each already visible in the corpus).**

| # | Entry architecture | Where the entry state comes from | Fold's domain | Correspondence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I-1** | **derived entry** | the fold computes `active` from `established` + per-kind promotion conditions | includes the entry term | NAE-25(a); ECI-23(i) |
| **I-2** | **given entry** | a lifecycle-side entry fact carries the state fixed at formation, after the condition was applied there | post-formation events only | ECI-17; ECI-31/32; ECI-23(ii) |
| **I-3** | **direct field** | formation writes the record's `status` with no lifecycle-side fact | no entry term; status partly mutable | NAE-25(c) |

**NAB-17 (Calculated — I-3 is the one the canon excludes).** K-R12 makes status "a **projection of that stream**, not
[mutable] authoritative fields" (`:111`), and ECI-17 requires the entry fact to be representable on the lifecycle
side. I-3 is therefore listed for completeness and is not a live option on the existing text; it is not a decision
this record offers.

**NAB-18 (Calculated — I-2 is the architecture that gives the clean separation).** Under I-2 the entry state is
established at formation and recorded lifecycle-side, and the fold's subject is **later** events. The promotion
condition is then a **formation predicate**, not a fold input. Neither Authority-E nor Authority-D enters the fold:
E is discharged before the fold starts, and D constrains the formation write, not the projection. This is the
restriction NAE-27 could not establish, and it is available **only** if I-2 is the architecture.

**NAB-19 (Calculated — I-1 makes the condition part of the fold, and the reading decides the consequence).** Under
I-1 the fold must evaluate something to produce `active`. If Authority-E, that something is a warrant recorded on
the event (attribution, corroboration) — a semantic predicate inside N-a, still no permission. If Authority-D, the
fold would have to consult **who was permitted**, which K-R14's independent rebuild cannot do from the stream alone
unless the permission is itself recorded. Recording it would require an event or field that does not exist — a
schema consequence, excluded by NSD-09 and A-6.

**NAB-20 (Calculated — the answer to Q1).** Fold semantics **can** be specified independently of the **permission**
that permits entry, in all three architectures, because permission governs the write gate rather than the fold. It
can be specified independently of the **entry condition** only under **I-2**; under **I-1** the condition is inside
the fold, and whether that is an N-a predicate or an imported permission depends on NAB-10's unresolved referent.
So the honest form of the answer is: **independent of authority-as-permission; conditionally independent of
authority-as-warrant; and the condition is the referent question, not DN-6a/DN-6b by default.**

**NAB-21 (Unestablished — which architecture holds).** ECI-07, ECI-08 and ECI-09 stand: §11 writes `provisional` at
formation and §8.1 writes `active`; the charitable reconciliation (promotion coincides with formation) and the
alternative (authored world facts are born `active`) are both consistent, and canon does not choose. I-2 does not
require ECI-07; it is compatible with both, because it fixes **where the state is recorded**, not **which state**.
Which state, and whether entry is uniform across kinds, remains unestablished.

## 4. Q2 — does N-a cover all three projected dimensions?

**NAB-22 (Source-derived — the domain is three dimensions).** K-R12: "a memory's current status, class and scope are
a **projection of that stream**" (`:111`). The stream is named as authoritative for all three.

**NAB-23 (Source-derived — but the kind list is status-shaped).** The same sentence gives the kinds as
`established | superseded | corrected | retracted` "plus `scope` and `knowledge_class` changes" (`:111`), and §5's
`status` field is `provisional | active | superseded | retracted | corrected` (`:322`). None of the named kinds is a
class change or a scope change; those appear only as a phrase.

**NAB-24 (Source-derived + Calculated — formation supplies class and scope; nothing supplies their changes).**
Formation writes `scope`, `knowledge_class` and attribution (`:604`; ECI-04), and §8.1 writes them again (`:404`;
ECI-05). The record carries them as fields (`:341`). So class and scope have **initial values** and **no change
vocabulary**. A post-formation class or scope change has neither a kind nor an input the fold could read.

**NAB-25 (Calculated — three possible semantic relations among the dimensions).**

| Relation | What it would mean | Inputs available today |
|---|---|---|
| **joint** | one function over one ordered stream produces a triple | status kinds only; class/scope have no change entries |
| **independent** | three functions over the same stream, one per dimension | same gap |
| **status-only** | the fold covers status; class/scope are not folded | the four status-shaped kinds are sufficient |

**NAB-26 (Calculated — the asymmetry, stated as a constraint not a preference).** Status-only is **statable over
existing kinds**. Joint or independent coverage of class and scope requires change events that do not exist, i.e.
new event kinds — excluded by NSD-09's "no substantive authorisation" and by A-6's "creates no event kind, field,
key or schema" (NAE-31). So the dimensions **can** have distinct semantics in principle — nothing forbids it — but
only one of the three has a vocabulary today.

**NAB-27 (Calculated — the domain question is therefore not neutral).** A usual "which dimensions?" question leaves
the answers equally expressible. Here one answer is expressible with the existing kind list and the others are not.
That is a fact about the text, and it is recorded as such; it is not a recommendation to choose status-only, and
choosing it would still be a **scope determination inside N-a**, not a fold shape.

**NAB-28 (Unestablished — whether class/scope changes are meant to be events at all).** K-R12's "plus `scope` and
`knowledge_class` changes" says they are stream inputs; it does not say they are recorded as events, and no schema
exists (ODA-12 via ECI-11). Whether a class or scope change is a lifecycle entry, a record edit, or something else is
nowhere stated.

## 5. Q3 — is the entry transition part of N-a, DN-6a/DN-6b, or an interface?

**NAB-29 (Calculated — entry decomposes into four components, not three).**

| Component | Question | Owner on the evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) representation** | is there a lifecycle-side fact recording entry, and what is it? | structural; constrained by ECI-17, not owned by N-a or N-d |
| **(b) state named** | does entry name `provisional` or `active`, and for which kinds? | **N-b** (vocabulary) + ECI-07/08 |
| **(c) warrant** | what justifies the advance for this kind? | **N-a** under I-1; **formation** under I-2 |
| **(d) permission** | who may cause it? | **N-d**, excluded; **not** DN-6a/DN-6b (NAB-12) |

**NAB-30 (Observed — (a) is already constrained).** ECI-17: the initial state "must be representable on the
lifecycle side" and cannot be inferred from acquisition events; ECI-31/32 find that L1/L2/L3 converge on this at
entry. This is a constraint the entry record already established, independent of ND-1 (ECI-40). It is not reopened.

**NAB-31 (Calculated — (b) is N-b's, and the scope determination already placed it there).** `active`/`established`
was absorbed into N-b (NSD-06), with `provisional`'s representation left unresolved (NSD-07, NSD-18). The choice
between entry naming `provisional` and entry naming `active` is therefore **not** an N-a decision, even though N-a
needs its result.

**NAB-32 (Calculated — (c) moves with the architecture).** Under I-1 it is a fold predicate over recorded warrants —
N-a's own content if Authority-E. Under I-2 it is evaluated at formation, before the fold, and N-a never sees it.
So (c) is **not stably N-a's**: its owner depends on a boundary decision that has not been made.

**NAB-33 (Calculated — (d) is the genuinely unowned component).** If §11's authority is deontic, "who may promote a
world fact to `active`" is a permission question. N-d is excluded (NSD-04). DN-6a/DN-6b are framed around forgetting
and retraction (NAB-12). Formation authorship is adjacent and open — Q7 asks "who may author an `ambient_authored`
record" (`:1024`) — but that is authorship of a record, not promotion of one. **No current item owns promotion
permission.**

**NAB-34 (Calculated — the answer to Q3).** The entry transition is an **interface**, but not the two-party interface
NAE-26 implied. It requires **(a)** a lifecycle-side entry fact, **(b)** N-b's state vocabulary, **(c)** N-a or
formation depending on the architecture, and **(d)** a deontic permission question **only if** Authority-D holds —
a question with no current owner. **DN-6a/DN-6b are not a party on the evidence.** The interface is therefore
three-way at most, and its fourth component exists only conditionally.

**NAB-35 (Calculated — this protects the scope decision differently than the previous brief supposed).** NAE-26
feared that N-a would leak into the excluded territory at the entry point. The evidence reverses the risk: N-a does
**not** naturally reach DN-6a/DN-6b, but a deontic reading would create an authority item that **NSD-04's exclusion
assigns to no one**. The scope determination is protected against leakage and exposed to an orphan, and the orphan
is created by a reading of one untagged sentence.

**NAB-36 (Unestablished — whether promotion permission is a lifecycle question at all).** It may belong to
formation/authorship (`:1024`), to a general lifecycle-authority item that N-d was meant to be, or nowhere. Nothing
states which, and this record does not choose.
