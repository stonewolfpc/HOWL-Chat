## 1. Scope and method

**DPD-01 (Observed — the task).** A dependency reassessment of the remaining open questions, read-only, before any
further owner decision. Nothing is opened as work, and the record does not say which question should be decided next.

**DPD-02 (Observed — why the order cannot be the record order).** DQ-3, DQ-4 and DQ-5 appear in sequence in GRP-53
because that is where they were listed, not because that is their order of dependency. GRP-54 already warned that its
own ordering is analytic and "does not imply that DQ-1 should be decided first". The same discipline applies here: a
dependency edge is evidence; a recommended order is a choice.

**DPD-03 (Calculated — "depends on" is used in one sense only).** A question **depends on** another when it cannot be
answered correctly without that other being answered first — because the second's answer fixes a term the first must
use. It does **not** mean: adjacent in a document, related in subject, or jointly interesting.

## 3. The dependency edges

**DPD-10 (Calculated — E-1: I-1 → I-7, ND-1 before DQ-4).** Source-derived from GRP-53's own table: DQ-4 "Is a
**validity basis** a separate obligation, and is ND-1 its prerequisite?" has "Depends on: **ND-1 (open)**", and it cannot
borrow from "the promotion path — its scope is promotion, and its **authority clause presumes ND-1**". A question that
asks whether something is ND-1's prerequisite cannot be answered while ND-1 is open, on its own framing.

**DPD-11 (Calculated — E-2: I-2 → I-3, predicate before set; analytic, not textual).** A set of admissible
transitions is the extension of a condition of admissibility. Without a predicate there is nothing to take the extension
of. **Neither canon nor any record states this order** — CEV-24 and LCM-24 record both as absent, side by side — so the
edge is **Calculated from the meaning of the terms**, not read off a provision.

**DPD-12 (Unestablished — E-3: whether I-1 → I-2).** Whether the validity predicate presupposes ND-1's answer is **not
stated**. GRP-31 lists the gap as "ND-1's, plus the missing predicate (CEV-24) and the missing admissible-transition set
(LCM-24)" — three components named together, without an order among them. One could argue a condition of admissibility
must include authority; one could equally define admissibility without it. This record asserts **no edge** in either
direction.

**DPD-13 (Calculated — E-4: I-4 → per-event compliance).** D-3b's obligation is per-event, not per-stream (TGP-11;
QAD-19). So without the statement-less boundary, no statement of the form "event *X* must carry a cause" and no
compliance assessment over the lifecycle stream can be completed: the class is undrawn and applies event by event.

**DPD-14 (Calculated — E-5: I-5 → the extent of ground retention).** Post-Q-β a ground is retained where the D-3b cause
is report-only (QBD-14). Without the report-only boundary, no extent follows — the classification is fixed and the
quantity is not (QBD-21; DRD-19).

**DPD-15 (Observed — E-6: I-6 has no blocking prerequisite).** DQ-3 asks whether a retained ground must be recorded as
stated and never evaluated. Its subject exists today — D-3b's cause is retained, and where report-only it is a ground

K15 precedent, whose scope is promotion and whose authority clause presumes ND-1 (GRP-53; GRP-46/GRP-47). A
**non-borrowing** constraint is not a dependency.

**DPD-16 (Calculated — E-7: I-7 is independent of every retention level).** GRP-53 records that DQ-4 "cannot borrow
from: any retention level — axis H is unchanged by all three (GRP-31)". So DQ-4 depends on I-1 and on nothing in the
retention space. Its independence from DQ-2 survives Q-β and DQ-2 itself.

**DPD-17 (Narrowed — E-8: I-8's present subject).** DQ-5 asks whether retention applies prospectively only. DQ-2 added
no requirement, so the only retention obligation in force is D-3b, which is an **existing** obligation applying to events
as they occur. DQ-5's operative subject is therefore narrowed to D-3b for the present set — and whether anything remains
to decide about it is **itself unestablished**. It becomes operative in full again if an addition is later decided
(GRP-36's asymmetry: a basis written after the fact is not a contemporaneous claim). Recorded as **Narrowed**, not
closed.

**DPD-18 (Observed — E-9: I-9 depends on nothing).** A prohibition is a new normative constraint requiring its own
