## 2. Detailed dispositions

Each entry preserves the wording of the accepted direction closely enough that its meaning cannot drift during conversion.

### 2.1 `OD-M09-01` — `R-6` silence satisfiability · **ACCEPTED**

`R-6` is **silence-satisfiable**. The governing construct is to be reconciled accordingly:

- The contradictory `CORPUS-01` L126 wording ("**No** — saying nothing also fails") is to be amended through a **separately controlled, dated standards amendment**.
- `R-6` is to be removed from `check_corpus.py`'s `POSITIVE_MODES`.
- `A-1` carries the anti-avoidance requirement: a configuration that passes `R-1`, `R-4`, `R-5`, `R-6` while failing `R-2`/`R-3` is **avoidance**, not restraint.

**Scope limit:** this records the **construct decision** only. `CORPUS-01` and `check_corpus.py` are **not edited** under this record. Until the dated amendment exists, the governing texts remain mutually contradictory and the record says so rather than pretending the conflict is closed.

**Known cost, preserved:** `R-6` is weaker as a standalone diagnostic under this reading; `A-1` carries the load. `MEMORY-09` L119 and `A-1` are consistent with this decision; `CORPUS-01` L126 and the checker constant are not, and must be brought into line by the amendment.

### 2.2 `OD-M09-05` — Membership authority · **ACCEPTED**

### 2.3 `OD-M09-03` — Cue standard · **ACCEPTED IN DIRECTION ONLY**

**Accepted:** the **layered development direction** — counterfactual screen → positive observable cue → historical attribution as supporting evidence → substitution deferred as validation.

**Not accepted:** `CORPUS-78`'s operational definitions and scoring mechanics as adopted standards. They remain **proposals**. `CORPUS-78`'s associated decisions (`D-OD9` … `D-R2W`) remain **suspended** under `CORPUS-80` §4 and are not revived by this record.

**Counterfactual judgments — three properties, kept distinct:**

| Property | Status |
|---|---|
| **Recorded** — rater states the counterfactual reasoning in the record | **Proposed requirement** |
| **Supported** — reasoning grounded in the item's declared evidence and defined counterfactual | **Proposed requirement** |
| **Reproducible** — another qualified rater applies the same rule consistently | **Unresolved validation requirement.** No threshold, κ value, or rater-study result is invented, implied, or claimed |

**`P-3`:** keep it **separate** unless a sufficiently specified **per-element** rule is actually written and justified. No merge is authorized for convenience; a merge without that rule would lose the distinct test `P-3` provides when a response contains both derivable and non-derivable elements.

### 2.4 `OD-M09-02` — Mode gating · **ACCEPTED WITH THE ANTI-COLLAPSE CLAUSE**

Mode failure may gate the item **only where the cue criterion is demonstrably distinguishable from the acceptance field**.

**The `F6-04`-style concern is preserved explicitly:** where the cue is itself part of the mandatory acceptance condition — as in `F6-04`, whose `mandatory` text *is* the perceptible accommodation — a separate mode result **cannot** be treated as independent without first resolving that overlap. `CORPUS-79` F-9 remains the operative finding, and `CORPUS-80` §4 recorded it as a premise.

**Not implemented here.** No item, field, tag or checker is changed.

### 2.5 `OD-M09-06` — Verdict vocabulary · **ACCEPTED WITH MODE-LEVEL ASSESSABILITY**

Two axes plus attribution, each reported separately and never aggregated.

**Acceptance axis**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not defined | No usable predicate has been specified |
| Not assessable | Predicate exists; required evidence unavailable under pre-response conditions |
| Pass | Evaluation occurred; defined acceptance conditions met |
| Fail | Evaluation occurred; defined acceptance conditions not met |

**Mode axis**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Mode not assessable | No adopted or sufficiently specified cue criterion exists, or its required evidence is unavailable |
| Mode not demonstrated | Criterion exists, evidence available, evaluation does not establish the required cue |
| Mode demonstrated | Criterion exists, evidence available, cue established per the adopted standard |

**Attribution axis**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Attribution not established | Available evidence does not establish memory as the cause |
| *(no "established" state)* | Would require a **validated causal-evidence channel and evidence sufficient to establish memory's contribution** to the observed response. Neither is established |

**Binding distinctions:** `Mode not assessable` must remain distinct from `Mode not demonstrated`. **No mode failure may be assigned where no adopted or sufficiently specified criterion exists, or where required evidence is unavailable.** Attribution must not be inferred from either acceptance or mode results.
