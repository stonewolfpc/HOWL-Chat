**DQD-16 (Calculated — P-G0's two halves now read differently, and neither is selected).** P-G0's claim has a

Recording that difference is not a rejection of P-G0; whether P-G0 is available as a **choice** is DQ-2's positional
clause, addressed in DQD-19.

**DQD-17 (Calculated — P-G1 describes the obligation the set contains).** P-G1's content is D-3b applied as written,
with no requirement added (PGR-12; GRP-25). Post-Q-β the description of what that artifact carries is fuller — cause,
report, and ground where report-only (QBD-06) — while the obligation is unchanged. So P-G1 describes the present
arrangement accurately as an obligation; its *name* remains a shorthand for the floor, not a new rule.

**DQD-18 (Observed — no position is selected, ranked or dropped).** The owner decided about the **requirement set**, not
about which retention position to adopt. P-G1's content **is** the existing decision rather than an option chosen over
others; P-G0 would require displacing D-3b and P-G2 would require adding a requirement, and neither displacement nor
addition was decided. So the decision does not select a position, does not rank one above another, and removes none from
consideration.

**DQD-19 (Calculated — DQ-2's positional clause, stated without over-reading).** GRP-53 asked "at which of
P-G0/P-G1/P-G2". With no additional requirement at any position, that clause has **no requirement branch to assign**;
