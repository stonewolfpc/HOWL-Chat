## 1. The decision as stated

**ODC-01 (Source-derived — owner).**

> "YES — selected.
>
> An actor-reported cause retains its identity as a cause while also being recognized as a report of that actor's
> stated rationale.
>
> The distinction is:
>
> * Cause: what produced or initiated the event.
> * Actor-reported rationale: what the actor says motivated their action.
> * Authority: whether the actor was permitted to cause the event.
> * Validity: whether the event was warranted under the applicable conditions.
>
> The reported rationale does not establish truth, authority, or validity.
>
> This is a classification decision only. It does not authorize a new field, schema, event type, carrier,
> requirement, or implementation."

**ODC-02 (Observed — provenance of the text above).** This is the owner's statement as supplied on 2026-11-20,
transcribed here without alteration. No persisted signature exists, and no canonical file was edited to record it
(ODC-39). It is quoted because it **is** the decision; every analysis that follows is labelled separately.

**ODC-03 (Source-derived — the four terms, as given).** The owner separated four things that the corpus had been
using interchangeably (TGP-02's five terms; CGE-07/CGE-08):

| Term | The question it answers | What it can establish, per the decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Cause** | *what produced or initiated the event* | nothing about permission or warrant — it identifies a producer |
| **Actor-reported rationale** | *what the actor says motivated their action* | nothing — it is a report of the actor's stated reason |
| **Authority** | *whether the actor was permitted to cause the event* | the permission question (ND-1, still open) |
| **Validity** | *whether the event was warranted under the applicable conditions* | the warrant question (no predicate, CEV-24) |

**ODC-04 (Calculated — what "retains its identity" forecloses).** The clause is a **prohibition on reclassification
in either direction**. A cause is not promoted into a warrant by being reported, and it is not demoted into "mere
report" by being attributable to an actor. Both readings hold of the same artifact at once. That is the sense in
which the owner's answer is neither of the two clean options the CGE record set out (ODC-13).

## 2. What is decided

**ODC-05 (Source-derived).** **An actor-reported cause remains a cause.** Its identity is fixed by what it does —
identifying what produced or initiated the event — and that identity is not altered by the fact that an actor
supplied it.

**ODC-06 (Source-derived).** **The same artifact is also a report of the actor's stated rationale.** The recognition
is additional, not substitutive: the record is read as cause **and** as report. Nothing in the decision makes the
report displace the cause, or the cause absorb the report.

**ODC-07 (Source-derived).** **The report establishes none of the three things it is most likely to be mistaken
for.** Not truth, not authority, not validity. This is stated as a limit on what the content can support, in the
form the owner used.

**ODC-08 (Source-derived).** **Authority and validity remain separate questions with their own answers.** They are
not answered by the cause, by the report, or by their co-location in one artifact. Their status is unchanged by this
decision: ND-1 open, no validity predicate (CEV-24; GRP-31).

**ODC-09 (Observed — the decision's own declared scope).** Classification only. No field, schema, event type,
carrier, requirement or implementation is authorised (ODC-01, last paragraph). This record adds none.

**ODC-10 (Inferred — what kind of statement this is).** The decision is an **interpretive classification of an
existing obligation**, not the creation of one. D-3b already requires the cause (*"Such events must retain **its
actual cause** and independently identifiable occurrence"*); this decision says what that retained content is and is
not, when the cause comes from the actor. It changes no text and adds no obligation. That is consistent with
GRP-23/GRP-24: D-3b remains M-1 (one retention obligation) plus M-2 and M-3 (two constraints on production), and
remains outside the retention axis the owner is choosing.

**ODC-11 (Observed — no requirement is created or implied).** The decision does not require that a rationale be
recorded, that a rationale be marked as reported, that a cause be tagged with its source, or that any reader check
one. It states what a recorded cause **is** when an actor supplied it. CGE-45's finding stands: this concerns the
classification of an existing obligation, not the addition of one.

## 3. What this resolves in the CGE record

**ODC-12 (Observed — the question it answers).** CGE-01's one question: whether the cause D-3b requires and the
ground the retention positions are about are the same thing under two names. The owner's answer addresses it by
declining the terms of the question: the cause is a cause; the **rationale** is a third thing, and neither is
authority or validity (ODC-03).

**ODC-13 (Calculated — the answer is none of CGE-43's three options).** CGE-43 offered (a) cause is a system report
of origin and never a ground; (b) a report-only cause **is** a ground; (c) leave it open. The decision is **not (b)** —
the owner did not classify the cause as a ground — and it is **not (c)**, because a classification was made. It is
also **not (a)**, which would have denied the report its standing. It is a **fourth position**: one artifact, two
recognised readings, all four concepts kept distinct, and the report's standing fixed as *not* truth, authority or
validity. Recording this explicitly so "*YES — selected*" is not read as an endorsement of (b).

**ODC-14 (Calculated — R-1 is unchanged).** CGE-20's answer stands: the cause is **not** a ground under another
name. The decision does not merge them; it adds a distinction rather than collapsing one. The three of four tests
that were independent of promotion material (CGE-15…CGE-20) are untouched.

**ODC-15 (Unestablished — R-2 remains unanswerable).** Whether one retained artifact could serve both readings is
still not settled, because the cause's shape is still open (SD-18: whether a field, a relation or part of an event
envelope). The decision makes the **double reading** explicit; it does not say whether the report is separable from
the cause within the record, and no carrier is authorised (ODC-09). CGE-02's R-2 answer — "no, depends on the
cause's shape" — is unchanged.

**ODC-16 (Calculated — R-3, restated with the new classification).** The retained-content question (CGE-02's R-3;
CGE-40) now has a determinate *character* and a still-undetermined *size*:

| | Before this decision | After |
|---|---|---|
| what P-G0 lacks relative to P-G1 | one artifact (M-1), whose status was ambiguous | one artifact (M-1), which is a **cause that also reports a rationale** — the ambiguity about what it *is* is resolved |
| size of the ground-axis difference | "an undrawn subset" (CGE-29/30) | unchanged — **still unmeasured**, because how often a cause is known only from the actor's account is not established |
| direction of the difference | uncharacterised | determined: the difference is in whether the report exists at all, not in whether it is trusted |

**ODC-17 (Calculated — P-G0's availability is *not* restored unconditionally).** CGE-35/CGE-36 held that P-G0's
coherence turns on the classification, because if the cause were a ground, P-G0 would have to displace D-3b.
The decision removes that particular trigger — the cause is not classified as a ground — so **P-G0 is not displaced
by this decision**. It does not follow that P-G0 is now fully characterised: P-G0 asserts *no ground retained*, and
whether "a report of the actor's stated rationale" counts as a ground in the sense P-G0 denies is **the residual
question** (ODC-18). That one question, and not the cause/ground identification, now carries GRP-21's consequence.

**ODC-18 (Unestablished — the residual question, named and left open).** Whether the **actor-reported rationale**,
as distinct from the cause, is a *ground* in TGP-02/GRP-09's sense — "what basis was claimed for this change",
first-person testimony authored by the party whose act it explains (TGP-20) — is not stated either way in the
decision. The owner described it as a report and constrained what it establishes; they did not label it a ground.
Two readings remain available and neither is chosen here:

| Reading | Consequence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| the rationale is not a ground — a distinct third content | P-G0 stays coherent as stated; P-G1's content is cause-plus-report | **not chosen** |
| the rationale is a ground of the weakest kind | P-G0 becomes a claim about vocabulary (GRP-21), as before, though now confined by ODC-07 | **not chosen** |

**Recording the residual question is not an invitation to answer it**: it is recorded so the record is not read as
having settled more than it did (ODC-31).

**ODC-19 (Calculated — what ODC-07 changes about the residual question's stakes).** The owner's limit — the report
establishes no truth, authority or validity — is **invariant across both readings of ODC-18**. So the residual
question's answer affects *vocabulary and P-G0's description*, not the authority or validity position of any
recorded content. The substitution danger TGP-19 described ("any recorded ground is the only candidate answer to
*were this warranted?*") is confined by the decision rather than by a new rule: a report that establishes nothing
is not a candidate answer, whatever it is called. **Axis H is unchanged** (GRP-31; CGE-37) under either reading.

**ODC-20 (Inferred — CGE-38 is confirmed, with the classification now fixed).** CGE-38 held that classification
changes the label and the constraint that attaches, not the artifact's standing. The decision fixes the label
(**cause**, with the report recognised alongside) and fixes the standing (**establishes nothing**). The two are now
consistent by construction, which is the substantive gain: previously the label was open and the constraint's
attachment was undecidable (CGE-44).

**ODC-21 (Calculated — CGE-39's gap is answered interpretively, not textually).** CGE-39 recorded that D-3b has no
"never read as warrant" clause, unlike CLV-28's explicit constraint on a ground. The decision supplies that
interpretation — a reported rationale does not establish truth, authority or validity — **for reading, not as
amendment**. D-3b's text is unchanged and no clause was added (ODC-09, ODC-39). So the gap CGE-39 identified in the
obligation's text **remains in the text**; what changes is that a reader is no longer without an answer. Whether the
obligation should eventually carry such a clause is **not decided here** and would be a textual change this
classification does not authorise.

**ODC-22 (Observed — the hinge moved; it did not dissolve).** The decision converts "is the cause a ground?" into
"is the actor-reported rationale a ground?" (ODC-18). That is a real narrowing — the cause is settled as a cause,
and the report is settled as establishing nothing — but it is not the disappearance of the question. Stating this
plainly is the difference between a classification and a closure claim.

**ODC-25 (Observed — no authority is created for the cause).** The decision does not make a recorded rationale, a
