## 3. What "separately tracked" means

**NSD-11 (Calculated — three questions, three subjects, no bundling).** N-a asks what a later lifecycle event does to
state; N-b asks how the status vocabulary relates; N-c asks whether the status stream needs an order key. Each can be
stated, examined and decided on its own terms, and none is a step in another's answer.

**NSD-12 (Calculated — the parts keep the relation they had, which is not a bundle).** **N-c is conditional on N-a** — an
order key matters only if the fold is order-sensitive, and DS-09 declined to decide whether it is (NDR-15). Tracking them
separately preserves that relation as a **dependency between parts**, not as an indivisible question: N-c's relevance is
settled by N-a, and N-c itself remains a distinct question.

**NSD-13 (Calculated — N-a and N-b remain separable in principle).** A mapping can be decided without a fold, and a fold
could be stated over a mapping adopted informally; neither entails the other (NDR-14). Separate tracking keeps both
available.

**NSD-14 (Calculated — why the split is the point, not bookkeeping).** DQ-2's precedent showed that a bundled question
hides which part an answer settles: its three parts had to be separated before it could be answered (DRD-02; DQD-08). The
same failure is avoided here **before** any semantic question is opened, rather than during one.

**NSD-15 (Observed — no part is opened as work).** Separate tracking names three questions and authorises nothing: no
examination, no option space and no decision is opened by this record (NSD-09).
