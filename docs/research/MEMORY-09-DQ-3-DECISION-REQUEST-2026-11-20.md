## 1. The question, and why it arrives as two

**D3R-01 (Source-derived — the question).** GRP-53's DQ-3: "If retained, is it required to be recorded **as stated and
never evaluated**?" Its stated dependency is "CLV-28; the K15 precedent only as analogy (GRP-46)", and it cannot borrow
from "the promotion path — its scope is promotion, and its authority clause presumes ND-1".

**D3R-02 (Observed — the condition is satisfied).** The question was written as conditional. Retention exists: D-3b
requires the cause, and where the cause is report-only the retained artifact is also a ground (QBD-14). So DQ-3's subject
exists today and no prior decision is needed to give it one (DSC-07).

**D3R-03 (Calculated — the phrase joins two constraints that govern different things).** "As stated" governs **what is
preserved**; "never evaluated" governs **what may be done with it**. They answer different questions, bind different
actors, and can be adopted in any combination (DSC-08). Presented together they read as one instruction, which is why the
request separates them.

**D3R-04 (Calculated — and only one of them can create an obligation of the "must be handled thus" kind).** Component A
imposes a fidelity requirement on recording; component B imposes a restriction on use. Either is an obligation if
adopted, so either answer of "required" changes the requirement set (DSC-05, DSC-09). A "not required" answer for both
leaves ODC-07 as the only boundary (D3R-10).

## 2. Component A — representation: "recorded as stated"

**D3R-05 (Calculated — what A asks).** Must the actor's stated basis be preserved **as the actor stated it** — not
paraphrased, normalised, summarised into a category, or silently corrected — and attributed to that actor?

**D3R-06 (Calculated — A's subject and its two failure modes).** A constrains the **recording**, and its two failure
modes are different: **rewriting** (the record says something the actor did not) and **anonymising** (the record loses
who said it). A fidelity requirement of this shape would have to say which of those it forbids, or both.

**D3R-07 (Observed — what already supports A's content).** The artifact is already read as a report of the actor's
stated rationale (ODC-06), authored by the actor whose act it explains (GRP-09: "first-person testimony about the act").
So A's content has a textual and decision-level basis; what is not settled is whether it is **required** as a constraint.

**D3R-08 (Calculated — what A can settle).** Whether the record must preserve the actor's own statement and its
attribution. If adopted, a record that substituted the system's characterisation for the actor's words would be in
breach. That is a statement about **form**, and it settles nothing about the statement's standing.

**D3R-09 (Calculated — what A cannot settle, and the limit the owner named).** **Deciding to preserve a report
faithfully does not authorise treating it as a valid reason for a transition.** A says nothing about sufficiency, truth,
authority or validity; ODC-07 and I17 govern those unchanged (`:305`). A reader could have a perfectly faithful record of
a reason that was inadequate — and faithfulness would make that easier to see, not harder.

**D3R-10 (Calculated — a "not required" answer).** That no fidelity constraint exists beyond what the report's
recognition already implies (ODC-06). It would **not** authorise rewriting: ODC-06 says the artifact is a report of the
actor's stated rationale, and a record that misstates it would not be one. Whether that implication is a constraint or
merely a description is itself part of what a decision would fix.

## 3. Component B — use: "never evaluated"

**D3R-11 (Calculated — what B asks).** May the recorded rationale ever be **evaluated** — and if not, evaluated for
**what**, by **whom**, and **when**? The phrase is unqualified as written, which is why its scope is a term of the
request (D3R-19) rather than a gloss.

**D3R-12 (Calculated — "evaluate" admits at least four distinct objects).**

| Candidate meaning | What is assessed | Relation to existing boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| **(i) sufficiency as a warrant** | whether the stated basis was an adequate reason for the transition | the object DQ-3 most plausibly names; CLV-28's inference is of this shape |
| **(ii) truth or accuracy** | whether what the actor said was true | **not** foreclosed by ODC-07, which limits what the report *establishes*, not whether anyone may investigate it |
| **(iii) compliance with a rule** | whether the basis satisfies some stated condition | presupposes a rule that does not exist (no predicate, CEV-24) |
| **(iv) scoring, ranking or comparison** | how one basis compares with another | no provision addresses it; GRP-35 records that grounds permit comparison of claims only against a rule |

The four are not interchangeable, and B's meaning depends on which it names. **This record adopts none.**

**D3R-13 (Calculated — B's subject is likewise a term).** If B binds the **fold**, it is a derivation constraint: the
cause/ground must not affect emitted state or be used as a decision input (the shape CLV-28 describes). If it binds a
**reader**, it is an interpretive norm with no enforcement (ODC-29: a limit is not a check). If it purports to bind a
**system check**, it would need a mechanism, and none is authorised (ODC-09).

**D3R-14 (Observed — CLV-28 is the closest existing statement, and it is an inference).** CLV-28's constraint —
referenced by the transition, never read as the status — is the fold-facing shape of (i). It is **not canon** and not
promoted; ODC-07 supplies the owner-level boundary covering the same content (QBR-22; QBD-16). So B, if adopted, would
mostly convert an inference into a stated constraint; it would not add a new prohibition on substituting a report for a
warrant, because ODC-07 already forecloses that substitution.

**D3R-15 (Calculated — the caution, as a term rather than a note).** A maximally read B would be a rule that no one may
ever assess a recorded rationale's adequacy — including under a validity predicate that does not yet exist. That reading
would let a constraint on **content** decide a question about **validity by accident**: it would foreclose part of axis H
without ND-1, without a predicate, and without any decision about either. Three objects must therefore stay distinct, and
the request fixes which of them B reaches:

| Object | What it is | Governed by |
|---|---|---|
| **(1) the recorded rationale** | a claim about why the actor acted | DQ-3's object; ODC-07's limit |
| **(2) the transition's validity** | whether it was warranted under applicable conditions | **no predicate exists** (CEV-24); ND-1 adjacent |
| **(3) any future validity analysis** | whatever predicate is later adopted, applied to transitions | not yet possible; not this question's object |

**A B that reached (3) would be a validity rule created without a validity decision.** The request states this so the
scope cannot be left implicit (R-5).

**D3R-16 (Calculated — what B can settle).** Whether, and within which of D3R-12's meanings, the recorded rationale is
barred from being assessed — and, if the answer is "not required", that ODC-07's boundary is the only constraint on its
use.

**D3R-17 (Calculated — what B cannot settle).** Whether any transition was valid (no predicate; GRP-31); who may cause
one (ND-1); whether a **later** validity analysis is permitted — which is object (3), outside B's proper reach unless a
decision deliberately extends it; whether grounds must be retained (DQ-2 settled); and any extent (QAD-19 and the
report-only boundary undrawn).

**D3R-18 (Unestablished — whether B is statable without a carrier term).** "Never evaluated" presupposes an identifiable
thing being withheld from assessment. The rationale's separability within the artifact is not established (ODC-15), and
no carrier is authorised (ODC-09). Recorded as a limit of the option, not a reason to answer it.

## 4. The components are independent

**D3R-19 (Calculated — four combinations, none recommended).**

| | **B not required** | **B required** |
|---|---|---|
| **A required** | faithful record, freely assessable | faithful record, not assessable for the named object |
| **A not required** | neither constraint; ODC-07 is the only boundary | record may be rewritten, yet may not be assessed — coherent only with an explicit scope term |

**D3R-20 (Observed — the fourth cell is not a reductio).** "Not required to preserve" and "required not to assess" is a
possible combination, not an absurd one; it is listed because presenting the two components as a bundle would hide it.
Nothing here recommends any cell, and the record does not predict which the owner will choose.

**D3R-21 (Calculated — no practical dependency either way).** A can be decided without B and B without A; each has its
own subject, and each changes the requirement set on its own. The only interaction is that a faithful record makes an
assessment of the actor's actual words possible — which is a fact about evidence, not an argument for either answer.
