## 3. `OD-M09-15` — S-1 through S-6 adopted

**Disposition: ADOPTED.**

The six requirements in `OD-M09-03` §3 are adopted as the standing design standard defining when a proposed cue criterion is **sufficiently specified**:

| # | Requirement | Prevents |
|---|---|---|
| **S-1** | Anchored | A cue any reply could satisfy |
| **S-2** | Distinguishable | `F6-04`-style collapse of cue into acceptance field |
| **S-3** | Pre-response | Retrospective cue-fitting |
| **S-4** | Directional | One uniform direction for blocking and inverted modes |
| **S-5** | Observational | "The model remembered" posing as an observation |
| **S-6** | Refutable | An unfalsifiable cue |

**Their wording is adopted as it stands and must not be silently weakened, strengthened, reinterpreted or replaced.**

### 3.1 What adoption establishes

A standard against which any **future proposed** cue criterion is assessed on all six requirements before it may be represented as sufficiently specified.

### 3.2 What adoption does not establish

| Not established | Consequence |
|---|---|
| No cue criterion for any existing item | The standard is empty of content until criteria are authored |
| No existing cue satisfies the six | No item is declared sufficient by this decision |
| No previously unassessable item becomes assessable | `F6-04` and every other unassessable item keep their status |
| No scoring, re-scoring, corpus change or evaluation | Nothing becomes runnable |
| No adoption of `CORPUS-78` §4.1–§4.5 mechanics | Proposals remain proposals |
| No adoption of any other proposal in the same document | Each stands or falls on its own |

**Do not claim any existing criterion has passed this test.** Doing so would require a separate, authorised item-level assessment, which this decision does not grant.

## 5. `OD-M09-17` — Reproducibility study required

**Disposition: TWO-RATER STUDY REQUIRED BEFORE PROMOTION BEYOND SINGLE-SOURCE CONFIDENCE.**

Cue-level results may be recorded and reported at **single-source confidence**. They may not support claims beyond it until a properly designed two-rater reproducibility study exists.

### 5.1 Mandatory distinctions

| Claim | Ruling |
|---|---|
| A single-source judgement may be recorded and reported at single-source confidence | **Permitted** |
| Repeated judgements by the same source become independent evidence | **Denied** |
| A second reading of the same material constitutes a second independent source | **Denied by default** |
| Different chat sessions, or conflicting model labels, establish independent model lineage | **Denied** |
| Independence and provenance may be inferred | **Denied** — they must be established and documented |

### 5.2 Study protocol — required content, before execution

1. Rater qualification and identity/provenance requirements.
2. Independent assessment procedures.
3. Blinding and the information available to each rater.
4. The exact judgement being compared.
5. How disagreements are recorded and handled.
6. The reliability measure and its justification.
7. Any acceptance threshold, specified **before** results are observed.
8. How insufficient sample size, missing judgements and unresolved disagreements are reported.

### 5.3 Limits of this decision

- It does **not** authorise executing the study, collecting new evidence, scoring, or any benchmark activity.
- It invents **no** reliability threshold and **no** statistic. Naming a measure is the protocol's job, not this record's.
- It does **not** claim reproducibility has been demonstrated. It has not been measured.
- It does not prohibit bounded **design** work on the protocol.
